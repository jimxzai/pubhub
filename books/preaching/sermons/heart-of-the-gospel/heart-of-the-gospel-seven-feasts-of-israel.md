# THE SEVEN FEASTS OF ISRAEL — God's Prophetic Calendar Pointing to Jesus Christ

## A Supplement to "The Heart of the Gospel" Sermon Series

---

## Introduction: God's Appointed Times

The Bible says in Leviticus 23 that God gave Israel seven feasts — seven appointed times, seven divine appointments on God's calendar. The Hebrew word is "moadim" — it means "appointed times" or "divine appointments." These were not random holidays. They were not cultural traditions invented by men. They were GOD'S appointments — dates HE set on HIS calendar from before the foundation of the world.

And here is what will take your breath away: every single one of these feasts points to Jesus Christ. Every feast is a prophetic picture of something Jesus would DO. The first four feasts were fulfilled at His first coming — to the EXACT DAY. The last three will be fulfilled at His second coming. And we are living in the gap between feast four and feast five — in the Age of Grace, the Church Age — right now, tonight.

Let me walk you through God's prophetic calendar. And as I do, I want you to see the precision, the beauty, and the urgency of God's plan of redemption.

---

## FEAST 1: PASSOVER (Pesach) — Leviticus 23:5

### Connection to Sermon 1: "The Cross — Where God Met Man"

The Bible says in Exodus 12 that God was about to deliver Israel from 400 years of slavery in Egypt. The tenth and final plague was coming — the death of every firstborn in the land. But God provided a way of escape.

He told each household to take a lamb — a perfect, unblemished lamb — on the 10th day of Nisan (the first month). They were to EXAMINE it for four days to make sure it had no defect, no blemish, no flaw. Then, on the 14th day of Nisan, they were to kill the lamb at twilight and apply its blood to the doorposts and lintel of their houses.

"And when I see the blood, I will pass over you" (Exodus 12:13).

Not when I see your good works. Not when I see your tears. Not when I see your religion. "When I see the BLOOD."

Now watch the precision of God. Jesus entered Jerusalem on Palm Sunday — the 10th of Nisan — the exact day the Passover lamb was selected. For four days He was examined — by the Pharisees, by the Sadducees, by the Herodians, by Pilate. Question after question after question. And every examiner came to the same conclusion: "I find no fault in Him." The Lamb was without blemish!

Then, on the 14th of Nisan — the exact day the Passover lamb was slain — Jesus was nailed to the cross. At 3:00 in the afternoon — the exact hour the Passover lamb was slaughtered in the temple — Jesus cried out, "It is finished!" and breathed His last.

Not one day early. Not one hour late. On the EXACT feast, at the EXACT hour, the EXACT Lamb of God was slain.

1 Corinthians 5:7 says it plainly: "Christ, our Passover, was sacrificed for us."

The blood of the Passover lamb saved Israel from physical death. The blood of Jesus Christ — the Lamb of God — saves YOU from spiritual death. From eternal death. From the judgment that is coming upon the whole world.

But the blood had to be APPLIED. It wasn't enough for the lamb to be killed. The blood had to be put on the door. A bowl of blood sitting on the kitchen table couldn't save anyone. It had to be applied to the doorposts — the place of entrance, the place of decision.

Tonight, the blood of Jesus has been shed. The Lamb has been slain. But have you APPLIED it to the doorpost of your heart? Have you personally received what Christ did on the cross? God says, "When I see the blood, I will pass over you." Is the blood applied to YOUR door?

## FEAST 2: UNLEAVENED BREAD (Hag HaMatzot) — Leviticus 23:6-8

### Connection to Sermon 4: "Sin — The Disease Nobody Wants to Talk About"

The day after Passover, the Feast of Unleavened Bread began. For seven days — a complete period — Israel was to eat NO leaven. More than that — they were to REMOVE every trace of leaven from their homes. They searched every corner, every cupboard, every crack with candles and feathers, hunting for even a crumb of leavened bread.

What is leaven in the Bible? SIN. Leaven puffs up, just like pride. Leaven spreads silently through the whole lump of dough, just like sin spreads through the whole life. Leaven corrupts from within, just like sin corrupts the heart.

Jesus warned, "Beware of the leaven of the Pharisees" (Matthew 16:6) — the leaven of hypocrisy, self-righteousness, and religious pretension.

Paul wrote in 1 Corinthians 5:6-8: "Do you not know that a little leaven leavens the whole lump? Therefore purge out the old leaven, that you may be a new lump, since you truly are unleavened. For indeed Christ, our Passover, was sacrificed for us. Therefore let us keep the feast, not with old leaven, nor with the leaven of malice and wickedness, but with the unleavened bread of sincerity and truth."

Now here is the prophetic picture: During the Feast of Unleavened Bread, Jesus's BODY was in the tomb. The sinless One — the One with NO leaven, NO corruption, NO sin — was buried.

Look at the matzah bread that Jewish families eat at Passover even today. It is striped — "By His STRIPES we are healed" (Isaiah 53:5). It is pierced — "They PIERCED My hands and My feet" (Psalm 22:16). It is without leaven — He was WITHOUT SIN (2 Corinthians 5:21). During the Passover Seder, the middle piece of matzah is broken, wrapped in a white cloth, and HIDDEN. Then later it is brought back out. Broken — His body broken for us. Wrapped in white — His burial cloths. Hidden — placed in the tomb. Brought back — RESURRECTION!

The Jewish people have been acting out the gospel every Passover for 3,500 years — and many of them don't even realize it!

Sin is the leaven. Jesus is the unleavened bread. And just as Israel was commanded to REMOVE the leaven from their homes, you are called to deal with sin in your life — not by your own effort, but by the power of the sinless One who was buried for you and rose for you.

## FEAST 3: FIRSTFRUITS (Bikkurim) — Leviticus 23:9-14

### Connection to Sermon 2: "The Empty Tomb Changed Everything"

On the day after the Sabbath following Passover, the priest was to take the FIRST sheaf of the barley harvest, bring it to the temple, and wave it before the Lord. This was the Feast of Firstfruits — the celebration of the beginning of harvest.

The firstfruits was a PROMISE. It was the guarantee that the rest of the harvest was coming. If the first sheaf was good, the whole harvest would be good. The firstfruits represented the WHOLE crop.

Now read 1 Corinthians 15:20: "But now Christ IS risen from the dead, and has become the FIRSTFRUITS of those who have fallen asleep."

Jesus rose from the dead ON the Feast of Firstfruits! The EXACT day! Not a coincidence — a divine appointment!

And just as the firstfruits guaranteed the full harvest, Jesus's resurrection GUARANTEES yours! He is the first sheaf waved before the Father. And if the firstfruits is risen, the whole harvest WILL rise.

1 Corinthians 15:22-23: "For as in Adam all die, even so in Christ all shall be made alive. But each one in his own order: Christ the firstfruits, afterward those who are Christ's at His coming."

There's an ORDER. Christ first — that's already happened. Then "those who are Christ's at His coming" — that's US! Our resurrection is as certain as His, because HE is the firstfruits and WE are the harvest.

The empty tomb on Firstfruits Sunday is God's guarantee that every tomb will one day be emptied. Every grave will give up its dead. Every believer who has died will rise again — because the FIRSTFRUITS has already risen.

Matthew 27:52-53 gives us a preview: "And the graves were opened; and many bodies of the saints who had fallen asleep were raised." On the very day Jesus rose, OTHER graves opened too! The firstfruits brought a sampling of the harvest with Him — a preview of what's coming for ALL of us.

## FEAST 4: PENTECOST (Shavuot) — Leviticus 23:15-22

### Connection to Sermon 5: "Born Again — What Jesus Really Meant"

Fifty days after Firstfruits — count them, fifty — came the Feast of Pentecost (Shavuot). The word "Pentecost" means "fiftieth." This feast celebrated the wheat harvest AND commemorated the giving of the Law at Mount Sinai.

Now watch God's precision again. Fifty days after Jesus rose from the dead — on the EXACT Feast of Pentecost — the Holy Spirit fell on the believers gathered in the Upper Room.

Acts 2:1-4: "When the Day of Pentecost had fully come, they were all with one accord in one place. And suddenly there came a sound from heaven, as of a rushing mighty wind... Then there appeared to them divided tongues, as of fire... And they were all filled with the Holy Spirit."

Wind and fire. The same two elements present at Sinai (Exodus 19:16-18) were present in the Upper Room. God was doing something NEW — writing His law not on tablets of stone but on tablets of the human HEART (2 Corinthians 3:3).

And look at this contrast: At Mount Sinai, when the Law was given, the people made a golden calf and 3,000 DIED (Exodus 32:28). At Pentecost, when the Spirit was given, Peter preached and 3,000 were SAVED (Acts 2:41). The letter KILLS, but the Spirit gives LIFE (2 Corinthians 3:6)!

The Feast of Pentecost had a unique feature: the offering included TWO loaves of bread baked WITH LEAVEN (Leviticus 23:17). Every other grain offering had to be without leaven. But these two loaves had leaven IN them. Why?

Because the two loaves represent two groups of people — Jews and Gentiles — both with sin (leaven), both brought together into ONE body by the Holy Spirit. 1 Corinthians 12:13: "For by one Spirit we were all baptized into one body — whether Jews or Greeks, whether slaves or free."

Pentecost is the birthday of the Church. It is the feast of the new birth. When the Holy Spirit comes upon you, you are born again — born of the Spirit, sealed, indwelt, empowered.

Jesus told Nicodemus, "You must be born again" (John 3:7). And Pentecost is where the new birth was demonstrated on a massive, public, undeniable scale. Three thousand people — born again in a single day. The Feast of Pentecost fulfilled.

Four feasts down. Fulfilled to the exact day at the first coming of Christ. Passover — the cross. Unleavened Bread — the burial. Firstfruits — the resurrection. Pentecost — the coming of the Spirit and the new birth.

Now look at your calendar. The first four feasts are SPRING feasts — Passover in Nisan (March/April), Pentecost fifty days later. Then comes a long SUMMER GAP before the final three feasts in the fall month of Tishri (September/October).

That summer gap is WHERE WE ARE RIGHT NOW. We are living between Pentecost (fulfilled) and Trumpets (not yet fulfilled). We are in the Age of Grace. The Church Age. The time between the first coming and the second coming.

The gap has lasted nearly 2,000 years. But it WILL end. The next feast on God's calendar is the Feast of Trumpets. And it could be fulfilled at ANY moment.

## FEAST 5: TRUMPETS (Rosh Hashanah / Yom Teruah) — Leviticus 23:23-25

### Connection to Sermon 6: "Revelation — The King Is Coming Back"

The Bible says in Leviticus 23:24: "In the seventh month, on the first day of the month, you shall have a sabbath-rest, a memorial of blowing of trumpets."

The shofar sounds! A hundred blasts of the ram's horn! The whole nation of Israel was commanded to stop everything and LISTEN to the trumpets. It was a wake-up call. An alarm. A summons.

In Jewish tradition, Rosh Hashanah (the Feast of Trumpets) is known as "The Day No Man Knows" — because it falls on the first day of the month, which was determined by the sighting of the new moon. Two witnesses had to confirm the new moon before the feast could begin. Nobody knew the exact day or hour — they only knew the SEASON.

Sound familiar? Jesus said, "But of that day and hour NO ONE KNOWS, not even the angels of heaven, but My Father only" (Matthew 24:36). The Feast of Trumpets is the only feast where no one knew the exact day — and Jesus said His return is the event where no one knows the exact day!

Now read these verses:

1 Corinthians 15:51-52: "Behold, I tell you a mystery: We shall not all sleep, but we shall all be changed — in a moment, in the twinkling of an eye, at the LAST TRUMPET. For the TRUMPET will sound, and the dead will be raised incorruptible, and we shall be changed."

1 Thessalonians 4:16-17: "For the Lord Himself will descend from heaven with a shout, with the voice of an archangel, and with the TRUMPET of God. And the dead in Christ will rise first. Then we who are alive and remain shall be caught up together with them in the clouds to meet the Lord in the air."

The TRUMPET! The next feast to be fulfilled is the Feast of Trumpets — the rapture of the church! The shofar will sound from heaven. The dead in Christ will rise. The living believers will be caught up. And we will meet the Lord in the air.

In the traditional Rosh Hashanah service, 100 blasts of the shofar are blown. The very LAST blast is called "the tekiah gedolah" — THE GREAT TRUMPET — one long, sustained blast. And Paul says we will be changed "at the LAST trumpet."

God told Israel to WAKE UP when they heard the trumpets. He is telling YOU to wake up tonight. The trumpet is about to sound. The King is coming back. Are you ready?

## FEAST 6: DAY OF ATONEMENT (Yom Kippur) — Leviticus 23:26-32

### Connection to Sermon 3: "The Age of Grace" and Sermon 4: "Sin"

Ten days after the Feast of Trumpets comes the most solemn day on God's calendar — Yom Kippur, the Day of Atonement. It is the day of covering, cleansing, reconciliation. The day the nation dealt with its sin.

On this day — and ONLY this day — the high priest entered the Holy of Holies, the innermost chamber of the tabernacle where God's presence dwelt between the cherubim on the mercy seat. He entered with BLOOD — the blood of a bull for his own sins and the blood of a goat for the sins of the people.

But there were TWO goats (Leviticus 16). The first goat was slaughtered — its blood was sprinkled on the mercy seat. This is a picture of Jesus shedding His blood for our sins.

The second goat was the "scapegoat" (azazel). The high priest laid both hands on the head of this goat and confessed ALL the sins of the people over it. Then it was led away into the wilderness — NEVER to return. This is a picture of Jesus CARRYING our sins away, removing them permanently.

Psalm 103:12: "As far as the east is from the west, so far has He removed our transgressions from us." That is the scapegoat — sin carried away forever, never to be found again.

The prophetic fulfillment of Yom Kippur is connected to the future repentance of ISRAEL as a nation. Zechariah 12:10 says: "And I will pour on the house of David and on the inhabitants of Jerusalem the Spirit of grace and supplication; then they will look on Me whom they PIERCED. Yes, they will mourn for Him as one mourns for his only son."

There is coming a day when the nation of Israel will look upon Jesus — the One they pierced — and will mourn, and will repent, and will receive their Messiah. Romans 11:26: "And so all Israel will be saved."

The Day of Atonement will be fulfilled when Israel as a nation recognizes Jesus as their Messiah. What a day that will be!

For YOU tonight, the message of Yom Kippur is this: sin must be dealt with. Blood must be shed. Atonement must be made. And it HAS been made — by Jesus Christ on the cross. Hebrews 9:12: "Not with the blood of goats and calves, but with His own blood He entered the Most Holy Place once for all, having obtained eternal redemption."

Once for all. The ultimate Day of Atonement. The ultimate sacrifice. The ultimate cleansing. The blood has been applied to the mercy seat of heaven. Your sins can be removed — as far as the east is from the west — TONIGHT.

## FEAST 7: TABERNACLES (Sukkot) — Leviticus 23:33-43

### Connection to Sermon 8: "What Will You Do With Jesus?" (Grand Finale)

The final feast. The Feast of Tabernacles — also called the Feast of Booths or the Feast of Ingathering. It comes five days after Yom Kippur and lasts for seven days, with an eighth day of special celebration.

During this feast, Israel was to build temporary shelters (sukkot) from branches and live in them for seven days. Why? "That your generations may know that I made the children of Israel dwell in booths when I brought them out of the land of Egypt" (Leviticus 23:43).

It was a feast of REMEMBERING the wilderness journey — and CELEBRATING that God brought them safely HOME. They were no longer wandering. They had arrived. The journey was OVER. They were HOME.

This feast was also called the Feast of INGATHERING — it came after all the crops had been harvested. Everything was gathered in. The harvest was COMPLETE.

Now here is the prophetic picture: The Feast of Tabernacles points to the MILLENNIUM and the ETERNAL STATE — the time when God will dwell WITH man on earth. The journey of human history will be over. The harvest of souls will be complete. God will tabernacle — DWELL — with His people forever.

Revelation 21:3: "Behold, the tabernacle of God is with men, and He will dwell with them, and they shall be His people. God Himself will be with them and be their God."

The tabernacle. God WITH men. The Feast of Tabernacles fulfilled in its ultimate, eternal form.

But here is what stops me in my tracks. On the LAST DAY of the Feast of Tabernacles — the GREAT day — there was a special ceremony. The priest would take a golden pitcher, go down to the Pool of Siloam, fill it with water, and carry it back to the temple in a grand procession. He would pour the water on the altar while the people sang from Isaiah 12:3: "With joy you will draw water from the wells of salvation."

And it was on THAT day — the last day, the great day of the Feast of Tabernacles — that Jesus stood up and cried out with a loud voice:

"IF ANYONE THIRSTS, LET HIM COME TO ME AND DRINK. He who believes in Me, as the Scripture has said, out of his heart will flow rivers of living water" (John 7:37-38).

The crowd was watching the priest pour water on the altar. And Jesus stood up and said, "I AM the water! Come to ME!" The feast was pointing to HIM. The water ceremony was pointing to HIM. And He interrupted the ceremony to make it personal: "Come to ME and drink!"

Now compare this to the very LAST invitation in the Bible. Revelation 22:17: "And the Spirit and the bride say, 'Come!' And let him who hears say, 'Come!' And let him who thirsts, come. Whoever desires, let him take the water of life FREELY."

The same invitation! The same word — COME! The same offer — living water! The same condition — whoever THIRSTS! From the last day of the last feast to the last chapter of the last book of the Bible, the cry is the same: COME!
