# THE PARABLES OF JESUS AND THE "I AM" STATEMENTS — Connected to "The Heart of the Gospel" Sermon Series

## Sermon 1: "The Cross — Where God Met Man" — Jesus's Own Words

### The Parable of the Vineyard Workers (Matthew 21:33-44)
The Bible says Jesus told a story about a man who planted a vineyard. He built a wall around it, dug a winepress, built a watchtower. Then he leased it to tenants and went on a long journey.

When harvest time came, he sent servants to collect his share of the fruit. But the tenants BEAT one servant, KILLED another, and STONED a third. The owner sent more servants — they did the same to them!

Finally, the owner said, "I will send my SON. Surely they will respect my son." But when the tenants saw the son, they said, "This is the heir! Let's KILL him and take his inheritance!" And they threw him out of the vineyard and killed him.

My friend, do you see? Jesus was telling His own story! The vineyard is Israel. The owner is God. The servants are the prophets — beaten, killed, stoned throughout the Old Testament. And the SON — the son is JESUS. Sent by the Father. Rejected by the people. Thrown outside the city walls and killed on a cross.

The cross was not an accident! It was not a surprise! Jesus PREDICTED it in this very parable. He KNEW what was coming. And He came anyway. He walked into that vineyard knowing full well they would kill Him — because the Father's love for you was greater than the Son's desire to avoid the cross.

And then Jesus asked the most chilling question: "When the owner of the vineyard comes, what will he do to those tenants?" The people answered, "He will bring those wretches to a wretched end!" They pronounced judgment on themselves without even realizing it.

What will God do with people who reject His Son? That is the question every person must face.

### "I AM the Good Shepherd" (John 10:11, 14-15)
Jesus said, "I am the good shepherd. The good shepherd gives His life for the sheep."

Notice — He did not say the good shepherd RISKS His life. He GIVES it. There is a difference! A hired hand might risk his life if the pay is good enough. But the good shepherd GIVES his life — completely, voluntarily, without reservation.

"No one takes it from Me," Jesus said in John 10:18. "I lay it down of Myself. I have power to lay it down, and I have power to take it again."

The Romans didn't take Jesus's life. The Jews didn't take it. Pilate didn't take it. Jesus GAVE it. The cross was not theft — it was a GIFT. The nails didn't hold Jesus to that cross — LOVE did. He could have called 10,000 angels. He could have stepped down. He could have walked away. But He stayed. Because HE is the good shepherd, and YOU are the sheep He came to save.

A hired hand runs when the wolf comes. He doesn't own the sheep. He doesn't love the sheep. He's in it for the paycheck. But the GOOD shepherd — He stands between the wolf and the flock. He takes the blow meant for the sheep. He sheds His own blood to protect them.

That is what happened at Calvary. The wolf of sin, death, and hell came for you — and the Good Shepherd stepped in the way. He took the blow. He shed His blood. He DIED so the sheep could LIVE.

### The Parable of the Lost Sheep (Luke 15:3-7)
Jesus said, "What man of you, having a hundred sheep, if he loses one of them, does not leave the ninety-nine in the wilderness, and go after the one which is lost until he finds it?"

Until he FINDS it! Not "until he gets tired." Not "until it gets dark." Not "until it seems hopeless." UNTIL HE FINDS IT. The shepherd does not give up.

And when he finds that lost sheep, he doesn't scold it. He doesn't punish it. He doesn't drag it home by the ear. The Bible says he "lays it on his shoulders, REJOICING." He puts it on his SHOULDERS — the strongest part of his body — and he carries it home.

The cross is the Good Shepherd leaving the glory of heaven — leaving the 99 angels, the worship, the perfection of paradise — to come down into the wilderness of this fallen world, searching for ONE lost sheep. Searching for YOU.

He searched through Bethlehem's manger. He searched through 30 years of poverty and obscurity. He searched through Gethsemane's tears. He searched through Pilate's courtroom. He searched all the way to Golgotha's hill.

And there, on the cross, arms stretched wide, He found you. And He laid you on His shoulders — those shoulders that bore the cross — and He REJOICED. "For the JOY that was set before Him," Hebrews 12:2 says, He "endured the cross, despising the shame."

YOU are the joy that was set before Him. Your salvation is what made the cross worth it. That is how much the Shepherd loves His sheep.

## Sermon 2: "The Empty Tomb Changed Everything" — Jesus's Own Words

### "I AM the Resurrection and the Life" (John 11:25-26)
Martha's brother Lazarus was dead. Four days dead. The body was decomposing. Martha said, "Lord, if You had been here, my brother would not have died."

And Jesus said the most audacious words ever spoken by any human being in history: "I AM the resurrection and the life. He who believes in Me, though he may die, he shall live. And whoever lives and believes in Me shall NEVER DIE."

Never die! Do you hear that? The one who believes in Jesus will NEVER DIE. The body may stop breathing, the heart may stop beating, but the PERSON — the real you — will never, ever die. You will simply change addresses. From earth to heaven. From the temporary to the eternal. From faith to sight.

And then Jesus asked Martha the most important question: "Do you believe this?" Not "Do you understand this?" Not "Can you explain this?" "Do you BELIEVE this?"

That's the question tonight. Not whether you can explain the resurrection scientifically. Not whether you have all the theological answers. Do you BELIEVE it? Because if you do, everything changes. Death loses its sting. The grave loses its victory. Fear loses its grip. And HOPE — living, blazing, unshakable hope — takes its place.

Jesus didn't say, "I know about resurrection." He didn't say, "I can explain resurrection." He said, "I AM the resurrection." It is not a doctrine. It is a PERSON. And that Person is alive tonight.

### The Parable of the Rich Man and Lazarus (Luke 16:19-31)
Jesus told of a rich man who lived in luxury — purple robes, fine linen, feasting sumptuously every day. At his gate lay a beggar named Lazarus, covered in sores, longing to eat the crumbs that fell from the rich man's table. Dogs came and licked his sores.

Both men died. Lazarus was carried by angels to Abraham's bosom — paradise. The rich man was in Hades, in torment.

The rich man looked up and saw Lazarus far away in comfort and peace. He cried out, "Father Abraham, have mercy on me, and send Lazarus that he may dip the tip of his finger in water and cool my tongue; for I am tormented in this flame!"

Abraham said, "Son, remember that in your lifetime you received your good things, and likewise Lazarus evil things; but now he is comforted and you are tormented. And besides all this, between us and you there is a great gulf fixed, so that those who want to pass from here to you cannot, nor can those from there pass to us."

A great gulf FIXED. Permanent. Immovable. Uncrossable. After death, there are no second chances. No transfers. No appeals. Where you are is where you STAY — forever.

The rich man then begged Abraham to send Lazarus back from the dead to warn his five brothers. Abraham said, "They have Moses and the prophets; let them hear them." The rich man said, "No! But if one goes to them from the dead, they will repent!" Abraham replied with chilling finality: "If they do not hear Moses and the prophets, neither will they be persuaded though one rise from the dead."

And there it is. Even the resurrection — even someone RISING FROM THE DEAD — will not convince a person who has hardened their heart against God's Word. Jesus DID rise from the dead. And still people refuse to believe. The problem is not evidence. The problem is the WILL. The problem is the HEART.

### Jesus's Three Predictions of His Death and Resurrection
Three times Jesus predicted exactly what would happen to Him:
- Matthew 16:21 — "From that time Jesus began to show to His disciples that He must go to Jerusalem, and suffer many things... and be killed, and be raised the third day."
- Matthew 17:22-23 — "The Son of Man is about to be betrayed into the hands of men, and they will kill Him, and the third day He will be raised up."
- Matthew 20:18-19 — "The Son of Man will be betrayed to the chief priests and to the scribes; and they will condemn Him to death, and deliver Him to the Gentiles to mock and to scourge and to crucify. And the third day He will rise again."

Three times. Each time more specific. Betrayal. Chief priests. Gentiles. Mocking. Scourging. Crucifixion. AND RESURRECTION ON THE THIRD DAY.

Jesus was never surprised. He knew every step of the way. The resurrection was not Plan B. It was THE PLAN. Planned before the foundation of the world. Predicted by the prophets. Proclaimed by Jesus Himself. And performed exactly as He said.

## Sermon 3: "The Age of Grace — The Clock Is Ticking" — Jesus's Own Words

### The Parable of the Ten Virgins (Matthew 25:1-13)
Jesus said, "Then the kingdom of heaven shall be likened to ten virgins who took their lamps and went out to meet the bridegroom."

Five were wise. Five were foolish. What was the difference? OIL. The wise brought extra oil for their lamps. The foolish brought their lamps but no oil.

The bridegroom was delayed. Midnight came. They all fell asleep. Then the cry went out: "Behold, the bridegroom is coming; go out to meet him!"

They all woke up and trimmed their lamps. The foolish said, "Give us some of your oil, for our lamps are going out!" The wise said, "No, there won't be enough for all of us. Go buy your own."

While the foolish were gone buying oil, the bridegroom came. The wise went in with him to the wedding feast. And the DOOR WAS SHUT.

When the foolish virgins came back, they cried, "Lord, Lord, open to us!" And he answered: "Assuredly, I say to you, I DO NOT KNOW YOU."

I do not know you. Five words of absolute finality. The door was shut. The feast was happening. And they were OUTSIDE.

The oil represents the Holy Spirit — the genuine work of God in your heart. You cannot borrow someone else's oil. You cannot ride into heaven on your mother's faith or your pastor's prayers. You must have your OWN oil. Your OWN relationship with God.

And the door that is open NOW will not be open forever. There is coming a midnight cry. There is coming a final moment. And when that door shuts, it shuts FOREVER. Are you ready? Is your lamp filled?

### The Parable of the Wedding Banquet (Matthew 22:1-14)
A king prepared a wedding feast for his son and invited guests. They REFUSED to come. He sent more servants: "Tell them I've prepared the feast — the oxen and fatted cattle are killed, everything is ready. COME!"

But they made light of it. One went to his farm. Another went to his business. Others seized the servants, treated them shamefully, and killed them.

The king was furious. He destroyed those murderers and burned their city. Then he told his servants: "Go out to the highways and hedges and invite EVERYONE you find — good and bad — to the feast."

The hall was filled. But one man came without a wedding garment — the robe provided by the king (representing Christ's righteousness). The king said, "Friend, how did you come in here without a wedding garment?" The man was speechless. And the king said, "Bind him hand and foot, take him away, and cast him into outer darkness."

The invitation is going out RIGHT NOW. God is inviting you to the feast. Some refuse because of business. Some refuse because of possessions. Some refuse because of relationships. What is YOUR excuse?

And notice — you must come on GOD'S terms. You must wear the wedding garment HE provides. You cannot come in your own righteousness. You must be clothed in Christ. Isaiah 64:6 — "All our righteousnesses are like filthy rags." Your best is not good enough. You need HIS robe.

### "I AM the Door" (John 10:7, 9)
Jesus said, "I am the door. If anyone enters by Me, he will be saved, and will go in and out and find pasture."

A door has two positions: OPEN or SHUT. Right now, the door is OPEN. Jesus is standing with arms wide, saying COME IN. "Whoever comes to Me I will never cast out" (John 6:37). The door of grace is open to everyone — rich or poor, young or old, religious or irreligious.

But remember the ten virgins. Remember Noah's ark. There is coming a day when the door will SHUT. And when God shuts a door, no one can open it.

"I am the door." Not A door — THE door. Not one option among many — the ONLY option. Acts 4:12 — "There is no other name under heaven given among men by which we must be saved." There is one door to heaven, one way to the Father, one entrance to eternal life. And His name is Jesus.

Come through the door while it is open. The clock is ticking.

## Sermon 4: "Sin — The Disease Nobody Wants to Talk About" — Jesus's Own Words

### The Parable of the Two Debtors (Luke 7:41-43)
Jesus was dining at the house of Simon the Pharisee when a woman — a known sinner — came in weeping. She washed Jesus's feet with her tears, dried them with her hair, and anointed them with expensive perfume.

Simon was disgusted. "If this man were a prophet, He would know what kind of woman this is!"

Jesus said, "Simon, I have something to say to you. A creditor had two debtors. One owed five hundred denarii, and the other fifty. Neither could pay. He forgave them both. Tell Me, which of them will love him more?"

Simon answered, "I suppose the one whom he forgave more."

"You have rightly judged."

Then Jesus turned to the woman and said to Simon: "Do you see this woman? I entered your house; you gave Me no water for My feet, but she has washed My feet with her tears. You gave Me no kiss, but she has not ceased to kiss My feet. You did not anoint My head with oil, but she has anointed My feet with fragrant oil. Therefore I say to you, her sins, which are MANY, are forgiven, for she loved MUCH. But to whom little is forgiven, the same loves little."

The deeper you understand your sin, the deeper your love for the Savior. Simon thought he was basically a good man — he owed only fifty denarii. So his love was small. The woman knew she was a great sinner — she owed five hundred. So her love was great.

If you think your sin is small, your love for Christ will be small. If you see your sin as God sees it — enormous, devastating, worthy of eternal judgment — then your love for the One who forgave that sin will be enormous, overwhelming, passionate.

### The Parable of the Pharisee and the Tax Collector (Luke 18:9-14)
Two men went up to the temple to pray. The Pharisee stood and prayed with himself: "God, I thank You that I am not like other men — extortioners, unjust, adulterers, or even as this tax collector. I fast twice a week; I give tithes of all that I possess."

The tax collector stood far off. He would not even lift his eyes to heaven. He beat his breast and said, "God, be merciful to me a SINNER!"

Jesus said, "I tell you, this man went down to his house justified rather than the other."

The Pharisee's prayer was a resume. The tax collector's prayer was a confession. The Pharisee compared himself to other people. The tax collector compared himself to GOD. The Pharisee was impressed with himself. The tax collector was broken before God.

Which man went home JUSTIFIED? The sinner. The broken one. The one who admitted the disease.

You will never be healed of a disease you refuse to diagnose. You will never be saved from sin you refuse to confess. The first step to salvation is the same as the first step to healing: admit you are SICK. "God, be merciful to me, a sinner." That prayer — seven words — is the door to heaven.

### "I AM the Light of the World" (John 8:12)
Jesus said, "I am the light of the world. He who follows Me shall not walk in darkness, but have the light of life."

Sin is darkness. Sin LOVES darkness. John 3:19 — "Men loved darkness rather than light, because their deeds were evil." Sin hides in the dark. Addictions thrive in secret. Lies survive in shadow. Guilt festers in silence.

But when the Light comes, the darkness FLEES. You cannot have darkness and light in the same room. Turn on a light and darkness vanishes instantly. It doesn't resist. It doesn't fight back. It simply ceases to exist.

That is what happens when Jesus enters your life. The darkness of sin, guilt, shame, addiction, fear — it FLEES before His light. "If we walk in the light as He is in the light, we have fellowship with one another, and the blood of Jesus Christ His Son cleanses us from ALL sin" (1 John 1:7).

Come out of the darkness. Step into the light. Let Jesus expose what needs to be exposed and heal what needs to be healed. The light may sting at first — just like your eyes hurt when someone turns on a bright light in a dark room. But your eyes will adjust. And you will see clearly for the first time in your life.

### The Parable of the Unforgiving Servant (Matthew 18:21-35)
Peter asked Jesus, "Lord, how often shall my brother sin against me, and I forgive him? Up to seven times?" Peter thought he was being generous. The rabbis said three times was enough.

Jesus said, "I do not say to you, up to seven times, but up to seventy times seven." Then He told a parable:

A king settled accounts with his servants. One servant owed him TEN THOUSAND TALENTS. In today's money, that's BILLIONS of dollars. An unpayable debt. Impossible. The king ordered the servant, his wife, his children, and everything he had to be sold.

The servant fell down: "Master, have patience with me, and I will pay you all!" He couldn't pay it in a thousand lifetimes. But the master was moved with compassion and FORGAVE the entire debt. Every penny. Gone.

That same servant went out and found a fellow servant who owed him a HUNDRED DENARII — a few thousand dollars. He grabbed him by the throat: "Pay me what you owe!" The fellow servant begged for patience. But the forgiven servant threw him into prison.

When the king heard, he was furious. "You wicked servant! I forgave you ALL that debt because you begged me. Should you not also have had compassion on your fellow servant, just as I had pity on you?" And he delivered him to the torturers.

Your sin against God is ten thousand talents — an infinite, unpayable debt. And God, in His mercy, forgives it ALL through the cross. But if you then refuse to forgive the hundred-denarii debt someone owes you — the offense, the hurt, the wrong — you reveal that you never truly understood the magnitude of your OWN forgiveness.

Unforgiveness after being forgiven is the most grotesque hypocrisy. If God has cancelled your billion-dollar debt, how DARE you hold a thousand-dollar grudge against your brother?

## Sermon 5: "Born Again — What Jesus Really Meant" — Jesus's Own Words

### The Conversation with Nicodemus — Deep Exposition (John 3:1-21)
Nicodemus came to Jesus at NIGHT. He came in the darkness. And that is more than just a time stamp — it is a spiritual picture. Nicodemus, for all his learning and religion, was in spiritual darkness.

He was a Pharisee — the strictest religious sect in Israel. He was a "ruler of the Jews" — a member of the Sanhedrin, the supreme court of Israel. He was "THE teacher of Israel" (John 3:10) — the most respected theologian in the nation.

And Jesus said to him: "Most assuredly, I say to you, unless one is born again, he cannot see the kingdom of God."

Nicodemus was confused: "How can a man be born when he is old? Can he enter a second time into his mother's womb and be born?"

Jesus explained: "Unless one is born of water and the Spirit, he cannot enter the kingdom of God. That which is born of the flesh is flesh, and that which is born of the Spirit is spirit. Do not marvel that I said to you, 'You must be born again.' The wind blows where it wishes, and you hear the sound of it, but cannot tell where it comes from and where it goes. So is everyone who is born of the Spirit."

The wind! You can't see the wind. You can't control the wind. You can't manufacture the wind. But you can hear it. You can feel it. You can see its EFFECTS — trees bending, leaves swirling, waves crashing. You know the wind is real because of what it DOES.

That's the new birth. You can't see it under a microscope. You can't produce it in a laboratory. But you can see its EFFECTS — a changed life, a new love, a different direction, a heart that was cold toward God now burning with passion for Him.

And then comes the most famous verse in the Bible: "For God so loved the world that He gave His only begotten Son, that whoever believes in Him should not perish but have everlasting life." The new birth is available to WHOEVER believes. Not whoever is good enough. Not whoever is religious enough. WHOEVER.

### "I AM the Vine" (John 15:1-8)
Jesus said, "I am the vine, you are the branches. He who abides in Me, and I in him, bears much fruit; for without Me you can do NOTHING."

Nothing! Not "without Me you can do a little." NOTHING. Zero. The branch has no life of its own. Cut it off from the vine and it withers, dries up, and dies. ALL its life, ALL its fruit-bearing capacity comes from the vine.

The born-again life is not a self-improvement program. You don't wake up one morning and decide to be better by trying harder. The born-again life is CONNECTED LIFE — connected to the Vine, drawing life from the Vine, bearing fruit because of the Vine.

A branch doesn't strain to produce grapes. Have you ever seen a branch sweating and grunting, trying to push out a grape? Of course not! The branch simply ABIDES — stays connected — and the life of the vine flows through it, and fruit appears naturally.

That's the Christian life. Stop striving. Start abiding. Stop trying to produce fruit by sheer willpower. Start staying connected to Jesus through prayer, through His Word, through worship, through obedience. The fruit will come. Love will come. Joy will come. Peace will come. Not because you manufactured them, but because HIS life is flowing through you.

### The Parable of the Sower (Matthew 13:3-23)
Jesus said a sower went out to sow. The same seed fell on four different types of soil:

1. **The wayside** — hard ground. The seed fell on the surface and birds snatched it away. This is someone who hears the Word but doesn't understand it. Satan comes immediately and snatches it from their heart. The Word never penetrates.

2. **Rocky ground** — a thin layer of soil over bedrock. The seed sprouted quickly but had no root. When the sun came out, it withered. This is someone who hears the Word with JOY — gets emotional, makes a decision, seems excited. But when trouble or persecution comes, they fall away. No root. No depth. Just emotion.

3. **Thorny ground** — the seed grew, but thorns grew up with it and choked it. No fruit. This is someone who hears the Word, but "the cares of this world, the deceitfulness of riches, and the desires for other things" choke the Word. They are too busy. Too distracted. Too in love with the world.

4. **Good soil** — the seed fell, took root, grew, and produced fruit — thirty, sixty, a HUNDREDFOLD. This is someone who hears the Word, understands it, receives it, and bears fruit.

Same seed. Same sower. Four different results. What makes the difference? THE SOIL. And what is the soil? Your HEART.

The new birth is what transforms the soil of your heart. Without it, your heart is hard wayside, shallow rock, or thorn-infested ground. With it, your heart becomes good soil — deep, fertile, ready to receive the Word and bear fruit that lasts.

What kind of soil is your heart tonight?

## Sermon 6: "Revelation — The King Is Coming Back" — Jesus's Own Words

### The Olivet Discourse (Matthew 24-25) — Jesus's Own Prophecy
Just days before His crucifixion, Jesus sat on the Mount of Olives overlooking the temple and told His disciples what would happen before His return:

"You will hear of wars and rumors of wars... Nation will rise against nation, and kingdom against kingdom. And there will be famines, pestilences, and earthquakes in various places. All these are the beginning of sorrows."

The BEGINNING. Just the beginning! Birth pangs, like a woman in labor. The contractions get closer together and more intense as delivery approaches.

"Then they will deliver you up to tribulation and kill you, and you will be hated by all nations for My name's sake." Christians persecuted worldwide? Check. More Christians have been martyred in the last 100 years than in the previous 1,900 combined.

"Many false prophets will rise up and deceive many." False prophets with millions of followers? Check. Turn on your television any Sunday morning.

"Because lawlessness will abound, the love of many will grow cold." A world drowning in lawlessness? Love growing cold? Check and check.

"And this gospel of the kingdom will be preached in all the world as a witness to all the nations, and THEN the end will come." The gospel reaching every nation? We are closer to this than at any point in history. Every day, more unreached people groups hear the gospel for the first time.

Jesus said these things 2,000 years ago. And they are happening NOW. The stage is set. The props are in place. The actors are in position. The curtain is about to rise on the final act of human history. The King IS coming back.

### "I AM the Alpha and the Omega" (Revelation 1:8, 21:6, 22:13)
"I am the Alpha and the Omega, the Beginning and the End, the First and the Last."

Alpha — the first letter of the Greek alphabet. Omega — the last. Jesus is the A and the Z. He is the beginning of all things and the end of all things. He was there when the first star was flung into space, and He will be there when the last star burns out.

History is not random. It is not chaos. It is not a meaningless cycle going nowhere. History is a STORY — HIS story — with a beginning, a middle, and an END. And it is all heading toward one grand climax: the return of the King.

He started it. He sustains it. He will FINISH it. "He who has begun a good work in you will complete it" (Philippians 1:6). The Alpha will be the Omega. The One who began your salvation will complete it. The One who started history will end it. And He will have the LAST WORD.

### The Parable of the Talents (Matthew 25:14-30)
A man going on a far journey called his servants and entrusted his property to them. To one he gave five talents, to another two, to another one — each according to his ability. Then he left.

The five-talent servant invested and made five more. The two-talent servant invested and made two more. But the one-talent servant DUG A HOLE and buried his talent in the ground.

When the master RETURNED — and notice, the master DID return — he settled accounts. To the five-talent and two-talent servants: "Well done, good and faithful servant! You were faithful over a few things, I will make you ruler over many things. Enter into the joy of your lord."

To the one-talent servant: "You wicked and lazy servant! You knew that I reap where I have not sown. You should have invested my money!" And the master took the talent away and cast the unfaithful servant into outer darkness.

The master is Jesus. The journey is the time between His first and second comings. The servants are US. The talents are everything God has given us — time, abilities, resources, opportunities, the gospel itself.

The King IS coming back. And when He returns, He will ask: What did you do with what I gave you? Did you invest it for My kingdom? Did you share the gospel? Did you use your gifts for My glory? Or did you bury it in the ground and live for yourself?

"Well done, good and faithful servant" — those are the words every believer longs to hear. Live today so that when the King returns, those are the words He says to YOU.

## Sermon 7: "The Bible — God's Love Letter to You" — Jesus's Own Words

### "I AM the Way, the Truth, and the Life" (John 14:6)
Jesus said, "I am the way, the TRUTH, and the life. No one comes to the Father except through Me."

Jesus is THE Truth. Not A truth among many. THE Truth. Not "my truth" or "your truth" — THE Truth. Absolute. Final. Unchanging. In a world drowning in relativism — where people say "what's true for you may not be true for me" — Jesus draws a line in the sand and says, "I AM the Truth."

The Bible is the written revelation of THE Truth. When you read the Bible, you are not reading human opinions or ancient myths. You are encountering the Truth Himself, revealed in words. "Your word is truth," Jesus prayed in John 17:17. Not "Your word contains truth" or "Your word points toward truth." "Your word IS truth."

In a world of lies, half-truths, propaganda, and deception — the Bible is the one Book you can trust completely. Because the One who wrote it IS Truth itself.

### Jesus's Use of Scripture Against Satan (Matthew 4:1-11)
After 40 days of fasting in the wilderness, Jesus was hungry. Satan came to tempt Him.

Temptation #1: "If You are the Son of God, command that these stones become bread."
Jesus answered: "IT IS WRITTEN, 'Man shall not live by bread alone, but by every word that proceeds from the mouth of God'" (Deuteronomy 8:3).

Temptation #2: Satan took Jesus to the pinnacle of the temple: "If You are the Son of God, throw Yourself down, for it is written..." (Satan quoted Scripture too — twisted, out of context!)
Jesus answered: "IT IS WRITTEN again, 'You shall not tempt the Lord your God'" (Deuteronomy 6:16).

Temptation #3: Satan showed Jesus all the kingdoms of the world: "All these things I will give You if You will fall down and worship me."
Jesus answered: "Away with you, Satan! For IT IS WRITTEN, 'You shall worship the Lord your God, and Him only you shall serve'" (Deuteronomy 6:13).

Three temptations. Three responses. Every single one: "IT IS WRITTEN." Jesus, the Son of God, the Word made flesh, the Creator of the universe — used the written Word of God as His weapon against Satan!

If JESUS needed the Bible to fight temptation, how much more do WE? If the Son of God quoted Deuteronomy to defeat the devil, how dare we neglect the Scriptures? The Bible is not just a book to read — it is a SWORD to wield. Ephesians 6:17 — "The sword of the Spirit, which is the word of God." Pick up your sword!

### The Parable of the Wise and Foolish Builders (Matthew 7:24-27)
Jesus said, "Whoever hears these words of Mine and DOES them, I will liken him to a wise man who built his house on the ROCK. And the rain descended, the floods came, and the winds blew and beat on that house; and it did not fall, for it was founded on the rock."

"But everyone who hears these words of Mine and does NOT do them, I will liken him to a foolish man who built his house on the SAND. And the rain descended, the floods came, and the winds blew and beat on that house; and it fell. And great was its fall."

Two builders. Two houses. From the outside, they may have looked identical — same size, same design, same neighborhood. But one was built on rock and one was built on sand. You couldn't tell the difference until the STORM came.

The storms come to BOTH houses. Being a Christian doesn't exempt you from storms. The rain fell on the wise man's house AND the foolish man's house. The difference is not whether the storm comes. The difference is what your house is built ON.

The rock is the Word of God — heard AND obeyed. The sand is everything else — human wisdom, popular opinion, cultural trends, feelings, self-help philosophy. Build your life on the Bible and you will stand. Build your life on anything else and when the storm hits — and it WILL hit — great will be the fall.

## Sermon 8: "What Will You Do With Jesus?" (Grand Finale) — Jesus's Own Words

### All Seven "I AM" Statements — The Grand Summary
Tonight, we bring it all together. Seven times in the Gospel of John, Jesus made "I AM" statements — each one a declaration of who He is and what He offers you. Together, they form the most complete self-portrait ever painted:

1. **"I AM the Bread of Life"** (John 6:35) — He is your SUSTENANCE. Without bread, the body starves. Without Jesus, the soul starves. He alone satisfies the deepest hunger of the human heart.

2. **"I AM the Light of the World"** (John 8:12) — He is your DIRECTION. In a dark world full of confusion, deception, and moral chaos, Jesus is the Light that shows the way. Follow Him, and you will never walk in darkness.

3. **"I AM the Door"** (John 10:9) — He is your ACCESS to God. There is only one way into the sheepfold. One entrance to heaven. One door to eternal life. And it is HIM.

4. **"I AM the Good Shepherd"** (John 10:11) — He is your PROTECTOR. The wolf of sin and death cannot snatch you from His hand. He laid down His life for you. He knows you by name. You are HIS.

5. **"I AM the Resurrection and the Life"** (John 11:25) — He is your HOPE beyond death. Because He lives, you will live also. The grave could not hold Him, and it will not hold you.

6. **"I AM the Way, the Truth, and the Life"** (John 14:6) — He is your PATH to the Father. Not a suggestion. Not an option. THE Way, THE Truth, THE Life. There is no other.

7. **"I AM the Vine"** (John 15:5) — He is your SOURCE of fruitfulness. Connected to Him, you bear fruit. Apart from Him, you can do nothing.

Everything you need, Jesus IS. He is your bread when you're hungry, your light when you're lost, your door when you're shut out, your shepherd when you're afraid, your resurrection when you face death, your way when you're confused, your vine when you're barren.

What will you DO with the One who is EVERYTHING?

### The Parable of the Great Supper (Luke 14:16-24)
Jesus said, "A certain man gave a great supper and invited many, and sent his servant at supper time to say to those who were invited, 'Come, for all things are now ready.'"

All things are now READY. The cross — done. The resurrection — done. The price — paid. The invitation — sent. Everything is ready. There is NOTHING left to do except COME.

"But they all with one accord began to make excuses."
- "I have bought a piece of ground, and I must go and see it." — POSSESSIONS
- "I have bought five yoke of oxen, and I am going to test them." — BUSINESS
- "I have married a wife, and therefore I cannot come." — RELATIONSHIPS

Excuses, excuses, excuses. Not terrible things — property, work, family. Good things! But good things that became an EXCUSE to reject the greatest invitation ever given.

The master was angry. "Go out into the highways and hedges, and COMPEL them to come in, that my house may be filled. For I say to you that NONE of those men who were invited shall taste my supper."

The invitation was rejected. The feast went on without them. And they missed it — not because they were terrible sinners, but because they were TOO BUSY. Too distracted by the good to accept the BEST.

What is YOUR excuse tonight? Business? Family? You're too busy? You'll get around to it later? The feast is prepared. The door is open. The invitation is in your hand. "Come, for all things are now ready!"

### Jesus's Final Words — A Timeline of Urgency
Let me leave you with the last recorded words of Jesus — a progression that tells you everything you need to know:

**On the cross:** "It is finished" (John 19:30). The work of salvation is COMPLETE. There is nothing to add, nothing to earn, nothing to improve. It. Is. Finished.

**After the resurrection:** "Go into all the world and preach the gospel to every creature" (Mark 16:15). The mission is CLEAR. Don't keep this to yourself. Tell everyone.

**At the ascension:** "You shall receive power when the Holy Spirit has come upon you; and you shall be witnesses to Me" (Acts 1:8). The power is AVAILABLE. You are not alone. The Holy Spirit equips you.

**In Revelation:** "Surely I am coming quickly" (Revelation 22:20). The promise is CERTAIN. He IS coming. Quickly. Soon. Any moment.

**The very last invitation in the Bible:** "The Spirit and the bride say, 'Come!' And let him who hears say, 'Come!' And let him who thirsts, come. Whoever desires, let him take the water of life freely" (Revelation 22:17).

COME. That is the last word of grace. Come. Whoever you are. Whatever you've done. However far you've wandered. COME. The water of life is FREE. The door is OPEN. The feast is READY. The King is COMING.

And the very last recorded words of Jesus Christ in all of Scripture are: "Surely I am coming quickly." And the response of every believer through every century, in every language, from every nation: "Amen. Even so, COME, Lord Jesus!"

What will YOU do with Jesus tonight? He has told you who He is. He has shown you His love. He has opened the door. He has set the feast. He has paid the price. He has risen from the dead. He is coming again.

The question is not what Jesus will do with you. He has already done EVERYTHING. The question is what will YOU do with HIM?

Will you come? Will you believe? Will you receive Him?

"Behold, I stand at the door and knock. If anyone hears My voice and opens the door, I will come in to him and dine with him, and he with Me" (Revelation 3:20).

He's knocking. Can you hear Him? Open the door. Let Him in. Tonight. Right now.

Don't leave this place without Jesus. Please.
