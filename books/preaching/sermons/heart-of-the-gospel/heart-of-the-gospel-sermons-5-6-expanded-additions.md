# SERMON 5: "Born Again — What Jesus Really Meant" — EXPANDED ADDITIONS

---

## A. Old Testament Typology

### Creation — Born of the Spirit (Genesis 1:1-3, Genesis 2:7)

"In the beginning God created the heavens and the earth. The earth was without form, and void; and darkness was on the face of the deep. And the Spirit of God was hovering over the face of the waters. Then God said, 'Let there be light'; and there was light."

Before creation, there was chaos, darkness, emptiness. Then God spoke, and light exploded into being. That is EXACTLY what happens when you are born again! Your life is formless, void, dark — and then the Spirit of God hovers over your chaos, and God speaks His Word into your darkness, and LIGHT comes!

Genesis 2:7 — "The LORD God formed man of the dust of the ground, and breathed into his nostrils the breath of life; and man became a living being." The first birth was God breathing physical life into dust. The new birth is God breathing SPIRITUAL life into a dead soul. Same God. Same breath. Same miracle. Different dimension.

Just as you did not create yourself physically, you cannot create yourself spiritually. The new birth is GOD'S work. John 1:13 — "Born, not of blood, nor of the will of the flesh, nor of the will of man, but of GOD."

Friends, I want you to think about that for a moment. When God made the world, He didn't ask the darkness for permission. He didn't negotiate with the void. He simply SPOKE — and everything changed. And when God decides to make you a new creation, He doesn't need your résumé. He doesn't need your credentials. He doesn't need you to clean yourself up first. He speaks into your darkness, and light comes. That is the miracle of the new birth. That is grace.

I have stood in cities all around the world — London, New York, Seoul, Sydney, Nairobi — and everywhere I go I meet people who are trying to create themselves. They are trying to build a life out of nothing. They are trying to manufacture meaning. They are trying to breathe life into their own dust. And I want to tell you tonight: you cannot do it. Only God can breathe life into dust. Only God can speak light into darkness. Stop trying to be your own creator and let the REAL Creator do His work in you.

The Bible says in 2 Corinthians 4:6, "For it is the God who commanded light to shine out of darkness, who has shone in our hearts to give the light of the knowledge of the glory of God in the face of Jesus Christ." The same God who said "Let there be light" at the dawn of creation is the same God who shines light into your heart when you are born again. It is a CREATION event. Not a self-improvement project. A creation. Something out of nothing. Life out of death. Light out of darkness.

---

### Ezekiel's Valley of Dry Bones (Ezekiel 37:1-14)

God showed Ezekiel a valley FULL of dry bones. Not just dead — DRY. Long dead. Hopeless. And God asked, "Son of man, can these bones live?" Ezekiel answered wisely: "O Lord GOD, You know."

God told Ezekiel to PROPHESY to the bones — to speak the Word of God to them. And as he spoke, there was a rattling, and the bones came together, bone to bone. Tendons appeared. Flesh covered them. Skin wrapped them. But there was still NO BREATH in them.

Then God said, "Prophesy to the breath." And the breath came into them, and they LIVED. They stood up — an exceedingly great army!

This is the new birth in picture form! The Word of God (prophecy) brings structure and form. But the Spirit of God (breath/ruach) brings LIFE. You can sit in church your whole life and have the structure of religion without the breath of the Spirit. You need BOTH — the Word AND the Spirit. That is being born again.

Now I want to ask you something tonight, and I want you to be honest with yourself. Are you a skeleton with skin? Do you have the FORM of religion — you go to church, you carry a Bible, you say the right words — but there is no breath in you? There is no LIFE in you? You look like a Christian on the outside, but inside you are dry bones?

I have met thousands of people like that. Good people. Churchgoing people. Moral people. But dead. Spiritually dead. They have the structure but not the Spirit. They have the form but not the force. And God is saying to you tonight what He said to Ezekiel: "I am going to put My breath in you, and you will LIVE."

The Hebrew word for breath is "ruach." It is the same word for wind, and it is the same word for Spirit. When Jesus told Nicodemus in John 3:8, "The wind blows where it wishes, and you hear the sound of it, but cannot tell where it comes from and where it goes; so is everyone who is born of the Spirit" — He was using this same word picture. The breath of God. The wind of God. The Spirit of God. Coming into your dead bones and making you LIVE.

You don't need another sermon. You don't need another Bible study. You don't need another church program. You need the BREATH OF GOD. You need the Holy Spirit to blow into your dry valley and make you stand up — not a pile of bones, but an exceedingly great army. That is what being born again means.

### Naaman the Leper (2 Kings 5)

Naaman was a great military commander of Syria — powerful, respected, wealthy. But he was a leper. All his greatness could not cure his disease. Sin is like leprosy — it doesn't care how important you are.

The prophet Elisha told him to wash seven times in the Jordan River. Naaman was FURIOUS. "Are not the rivers of Damascus better than all the waters of Israel?" He wanted a dramatic, dignified cure. Not this muddy little river.

His servants said, "If the prophet had told you to do something great, would you not have done it? How much more when he says simply, 'Wash, and be clean?'"

Naaman humbled himself. He went down into the Jordan — once, twice, three times... nothing changed. Four, five, six times — still leprous. On the SEVENTH time, his flesh was restored "like the flesh of a little child." Like a CHILD. Born again!

The new birth requires humility. You must come God's way, not your way. You must humble yourself like Naaman. And God's way is always simpler than you expect — not your works, not your greatness, but simple faith in His Word.

I want you to notice something. Naaman had a problem with God's method. He had no problem with the GOAL — he wanted to be healed. But he had a problem with the WAY God chose to do it. And I find that is true of so many people. They want to be saved. They want to go to heaven. They want peace with God. But they want to come their OWN way. They want to earn it. They want to deserve it. They want a way that makes THEM look good.

And God says: No. My way. The Jordan. The cross. The blood. Simple faith. Not your achievements. Not your intellect. Not your bank account. Just humble yourself and wash.

The Bible says in 1 Corinthians 1:27-29, "God has chosen the foolish things of the world to put to shame the wise, and God has chosen the weak things of the world to put to shame the things which are mighty... that no flesh should glory in His presence." God deliberately chose a simple way so that no one could boast. The new birth is not for the elite. It is for anyone humble enough to come God's way.

And notice that Naaman's flesh was restored like that of a little child. Not like a general. Not like a commander. Like a CHILD. Jesus said, "Unless you are converted and become as little children, you will by no means enter the kingdom of heaven" (Matthew 18:3). The new birth makes generals into children. It makes the powerful into the humble. It makes the proud into the dependent. And that is exactly where God wants you — dependent on Him, like a little child depends on a father.

---

### The Exodus — From Slavery to Freedom (Exodus 12-14)

Israel was in bondage in Egypt for 400 years. They could not free themselves. No amount of effort, education, or self-improvement could break Pharaoh's chains. They needed DELIVERANCE from OUTSIDE.

The blood of the Passover lamb protected them. The Red Sea opened before them. They passed through the waters (a picture of baptism — 1 Corinthians 10:1-2) and came out on the OTHER SIDE as a FREE people. Old life behind them. New life ahead.

The new birth is your personal Exodus. You were in bondage to sin. The blood of Jesus (the Lamb of God) protects you. You pass through the waters of the new birth and emerge on the other side FREE. "If the Son makes you free, you shall be free indeed" (John 8:36).

Think about this. The Israelites did not fight their way out of Egypt. They did not negotiate. They did not earn their freedom. God DELIVERED them. And when they stood on the other side of the Red Sea and looked back, the waters had closed over their enemies. The army that had pursued them was gone. Destroyed. Finished.

When you are born again, the sins that pursued you — the guilt, the shame, the bondage, the addiction, the patterns that had you in chains — God closes the waters over them. They are GONE. The old life is behind you. You are standing on the other side, and you are free.

The Bible says in Romans 6:6-7, "Knowing this, that our old man was crucified with Him, that the body of sin might be done away with, that we should no longer be slaves of sin. For he who has died has been freed from sin." The new birth is not a cosmetic change. It is an Exodus. It is a liberation. It is the breaking of chains that have held you for years — maybe for your entire life.

And I want you to hear this: it doesn't matter how long you've been in bondage. Israel was in Egypt for 400 years! Four hundred years of slavery. And God broke those chains in a SINGLE NIGHT. The night of the Passover. The night the blood was applied. Friend, God can break your chains tonight. He can set you free tonight. Not next year. Not after you've tried harder. TONIGHT. One encounter with the blood of the Lamb, and your Exodus begins.

## B. Additional Pauline Connections

### Romans 8:1-17 — Life in the Spirit

"There is therefore now NO condemnation to those who are in Christ Jesus." The born-again person is free from condemnation. Not reduced condemnation. NO condemnation. The slate is wiped clean.

Romans 8:9 — "If anyone does not have the Spirit of Christ, he is not His." The new birth is receiving the Holy Spirit. Without the Spirit, you do not belong to Christ. With the Spirit, you are His forever.

Romans 8:14-16 — "For as many as are led by the Spirit of God, these are sons of God... The Spirit Himself bears witness with our spirit that we are children of God." How do you know you're born again? The Spirit tells you. Deep inside, the Spirit of God whispers to your spirit: "You are Mine. You are a child of God." That inner witness is the evidence of the new birth.

I cannot tell you how many people have come to me over the years and said, "Billy, how can I KNOW? How can I be SURE I'm born again?" And I point them to Romans 8:16. The Spirit bears witness. It is not a feeling — though there may be feelings. It is not an emotion — though there may be emotion. It is a deep, settled knowing. A conviction in your innermost being that you belong to God. The orphan spirit is gone, and the spirit of sonship has come. You KNOW you are His.

The Bible says in Romans 8:1, "NO condemnation." Let those words wash over you. If you are in Christ Jesus, there is NO condemnation. Not "less condemnation." Not "condemnation that comes and goes." NO condemnation. None. Zero. The Judge of the universe has declared you NOT GUILTY — not because you are innocent, but because the penalty has been PAID. That is what the new birth secures for you. That is what it means to be in Christ.

---

### Galatians 4:4-7 — Adoption as Sons

"God sent forth His Son... that we might receive the adoption as sons. And because you are sons, God has sent forth the Spirit of His Son into your hearts, crying out, 'Abba, Father!'"

When you are born again, God adopts you. You go from slave to SON. From outsider to FAMILY. And the Spirit within you cries "Abba" — Daddy! Papa! The most intimate term of relationship. You don't call a stranger "Daddy." Only a child calls out "Abba."

I remember holding my own children when they were small, and they would call out, "Daddy!" And something in my heart would leap. That is what happens in the heart of God when you, His born-again child, cry out "Abba, Father!" God's heart leaps. You are not a stranger petitioning a distant deity. You are a child running into your Father's arms. That is the new birth. That is what Jesus made possible.

---

### Ephesians 2:4-10 — God's Workmanship

"By grace you have been saved through faith, and that not of yourselves; it is the gift of God, not of works, lest anyone should boast. For we are His workmanship, created in Christ Jesus for good works."

The born-again person is God's WORKMANSHIP — His masterpiece, His poem (the Greek word is "poiema," from which we get "poem"). You are God's work of art. Not your self-improvement project. GOD'S masterpiece.

You know, the world will tell you that you are an accident. A random collection of atoms. A product of blind chance. But the Bible says you are God's POEM. His poiema. His masterpiece. And when you are born again, God begins writing the most beautiful story ever told — the story of His grace in your life. Every chapter, every verse, every stanza — authored by God Himself. You are not a rough draft. You are not a mistake. You are a work in progress by the greatest Artist in the universe.

---

### Philippians 3:3-11 — Paul's Credentials vs. Christ

Paul had the most impressive religious résumé in history: circumcised the eighth day, tribe of Benjamin, Pharisee, zealous, blameless under the law. If anyone could have earned their way, it was Paul.

But he said: "What things were gain to me, these I have counted LOSS for Christ... I count them as RUBBISH, that I may gain Christ." The Greek word for "rubbish" is "skubalon" — it means garbage, refuse, dung. All his religion, all his achievement, all his pedigree — GARBAGE compared to knowing Christ.

The new birth is not adding religion to your life. It is exchanging your life for Christ's life. It is counting everything else as loss.

If the apostle Paul — the greatest Christian who ever lived — said that all his religious credentials were garbage, what does that say about YOURS? What does that say about your church membership? Your baptism certificate? Your moral record? Your charitable giving? Paul says: garbage. Rubbish. Dung. Compared to KNOWING CHRIST.

The new birth is not about what you bring to the table. It is about what Christ brings to YOU. It is not about your qualifications. It is about His grace. And until you are willing to count everything else as loss — as Paul did — you will never truly know the power of the new birth.

---

### Colossians 3:1-4 — Raised with Christ

"If then you were raised with Christ, seek those things which are above... For you died, and your life is hidden with Christ in God."

Born again means your old self DIED and a new self was RAISED. Your life is now "hidden with Christ in God." You are tucked away in the safest place in the universe — in Christ, in God. No one can snatch you out.

I love that phrase: "hidden with Christ in God." Think of a treasure locked inside a safe, and that safe locked inside a vault. You are in Christ, and Christ is in God. Double security. The devil cannot reach you. The world cannot touch you. Your life is HIDDEN — secure, protected, preserved. Not because of your grip on God, but because of God's grip on YOU.

---

### Titus 3:4-7 — The Washing of Regeneration

"Not by works of righteousness which we have done, but according to His mercy He saved us, through the washing of regeneration and renewing of the Holy Spirit."

The new birth is called "regeneration" — re-generation, being generated again. And it comes through the Holy Spirit, not through your efforts. You are washed clean and made new. Not reformed. Not improved. REGENERATED.

That word "regeneration" is used only twice in the entire New Testament. Once here in Titus, and once in Matthew 19:28 where Jesus speaks of the regeneration of the whole world at His return. Your personal regeneration at the new birth is a preview of the cosmic regeneration that is coming. You are the firstfruits of a new creation. When God regenerates you, He is giving you — and the watching world — a preview of what He is going to do to the entire universe.

## C. Practical Application: Evidence of the New Birth

### How Do You Know You Are Born Again?

1. **You have a new love for God** — 1 John 4:19: "We love Him because He first loved us." Before the new birth, God was distant. After, He is Father. You find yourself wanting to pray, wanting to worship, wanting to be near God. That desire is not natural — it is supernatural. It is evidence of the new birth.

2. **You have a new love for other believers** — 1 John 3:14: "We know that we have passed from death to life, because we love the brethren." You suddenly feel a connection to people you've never met — because they share the same Father. The family of God becomes YOUR family. That love is evidence.

3. **You have a new hatred of sin** — 1 John 3:9: "Whoever has been born of God does not practice sin." You still sin, but you no longer love it. It grieves you. Before the new birth, sin was fun. After the new birth, sin is painful. That change is evidence.

4. **You have a new desire for God's Word** — 1 Peter 2:2: "As newborn babes, desire the pure milk of the word, that you may grow thereby." The Bible becomes alive. What was boring becomes fascinating. What was confusing becomes clear. That hunger is evidence.

5. **You have the inner witness of the Spirit** — Romans 8:16: "The Spirit Himself bears witness with our spirit that we are children of God." Deep in your heart, you KNOW. Not because someone told you. Not because you signed a card. Because the Spirit of God confirms it in your innermost being.

6. **Your life produces fruit** — Galatians 5:22-23: Love, joy, peace, patience, kindness, goodness, faithfulness, gentleness, self-control. Not perfection — but FRUIT. Growth. Change. Transformation that others can see.

7. **You overcome the world** — 1 John 5:4: "Whatever is born of God overcomes the world." The pull of the world grows weaker. The things that once controlled you begin to lose their grip. You are an overcomer — not by your strength, but by the power of the new birth within you.

---

### Common Misconceptions About Being Born Again

- **"I was born into a Christian family"** — Nicodemus was born into the most religious family possible. Jesus still told him: "You MUST be born again." Christianity is not inherited. Faith is not genetic. Each person must be born again individually.

- **"I was baptized as a baby"** — Baptism is an outward sign, not the new birth itself. Simon the sorcerer was baptized but was not truly born again (Acts 8:13-23). Baptism is important, but it is not the same as regeneration.

- **"I made a decision at a church event"** — A decision is not the same as regeneration. Many make decisions who are never truly born again. The new birth produces lasting change, not just a momentary emotion. If nothing changed after your "decision," you may need to truly be born again.

- **"I try to be a good person"** — Nicodemus WAS a good person. Morality is not the new birth. You can be moral and still be spiritually dead. The Pharisees were the most moral people in Israel, and Jesus said they were whitewashed tombs — beautiful on the outside, full of dead bones on the inside.

Let me say it as plainly as I can: being born again is not something YOU do. It is something GOD does. You can no more birth yourself spiritually than you could birth yourself physically. But you CAN respond to God's call. You can come to Him. You can open the door of your heart. And when you do, HE does the miracle of the new birth.

## D. Broader Revelation Connections

### Revelation 2:7 — Overcomers and the Tree of Life

"To him who overcomes I will give to eat from the tree of life, which is in the midst of the Paradise of God." The overcomer is the born-again believer (1 John 5:4-5). The reward is access to the tree of life that was lost in Eden. What Adam lost, Christ restores.

Think about that. In Genesis 3, God placed cherubim and a flaming sword to guard the way to the tree of life. Man was barred from it because of sin. But in Revelation 2, Jesus promises the overcomer ACCESS to that tree. The sword is removed. The cherubim step aside. The born-again believer walks right up to the tree of life and EATS. What was lost in the first garden is restored in the last. That is the power of the new birth — it reverses the curse.

### Revelation 3:5 — The Book of Life

"He who overcomes shall be clothed in white garments, and I will not blot out his name from the Book of Life; but I will confess his name before My Father and before His angels." The born-again believer's name is written in the Lamb's Book of Life. Secure. Permanent. Confessed by Christ before the Father.

Imagine standing before the throne of God, and Jesus Christ Himself turns to the Father and says YOUR name. "This one is Mine. I know them. Their name is in My book." That is what the new birth guarantees. Jesus will CONFESS you before the Father. Not because of what you've done, but because of what HE did. Because you are born again.

### Revelation 21:5 — All Things New

"Behold, I make ALL things new." The new birth is the beginning of God's "making all things new." It starts with YOU — a new creation (2 Corinthians 5:17). And it will culminate with a new heaven and a new earth. God is in the business of making things new. Let Him start with you.

Tonight, God is offering you the new birth. Not religion. Not self-improvement. Not a New Year's resolution. A new LIFE. A new heart. A new beginning. The Spirit of God is hovering over the chaos of your life right now, and God is ready to speak: "Let there be light." Will you let Him?

# SERMON 6: "Revelation — The King Is Coming Back" — EXPANDED ADDITIONS

---

## A. Old Testament Typology

### Daniel's Vision of the Son of Man (Daniel 7:13-14)

"I was watching in the night visions, and behold, One like the Son of Man, coming with the clouds of heaven! He came to the Ancient of Days, and they brought Him near before Him. Then to Him was given dominion and glory and a kingdom, that all peoples, nations, and languages should serve Him. His dominion is an everlasting dominion, which shall not pass away."

600 years before Jesus was born, Daniel SAW Him coming in glory. Not as a baby in a manger. Not as a suffering servant. But as a KING with dominion, glory, and an everlasting kingdom. The first time Jesus came, He fulfilled Isaiah 53 — the suffering servant. The second time, He fulfills Daniel 7 — the reigning King.

My friends, I want you to understand something tonight. When Jesus walked the dusty roads of Galilee, when He was mocked and spit upon, when they pressed that crown of thorns into His brow, when they nailed Him to that cross — He KNEW that Daniel 7 was still ahead of Him. He endured the cross for the joy that was set before Him (Hebrews 12:2). He knew that the day was coming when every nation, every tribe, every tongue would bow before Him. The suffering was temporary. The glory is PERMANENT.

And the Bible says His dominion "shall not pass away." Every other kingdom has an expiration date. Babylon fell. Persia fell. Greece fell. Rome fell. The British Empire fell. The Soviet Union fell. Every earthly kingdom rises and falls. But the kingdom of Jesus Christ shall NOT pass away. It is the only kingdom worth investing in. The only kingdom that will still be standing when the dust of history settles.

---

### Daniel's Statue and the Stone (Daniel 2:31-45)

Nebuchadnezzar dreamed of a great statue: head of gold (Babylon), chest of silver (Medo-Persia), belly of bronze (Greece), legs of iron (Rome), feet of iron and clay (the final world empire).

Then a STONE was cut out "without hands" — it struck the statue on its feet and the whole thing crumbled to dust. And the stone became a great mountain that filled the WHOLE EARTH.

That stone is Jesus Christ. Not carved by human hands — born of a virgin by the Holy Spirit. He will strike the kingdoms of this world and they will crumble. And HIS kingdom will fill the whole earth. Every dictator, every empire, every government that opposes God will be crushed by that Stone.

I have met with presidents and prime ministers. I have sat in the palaces of kings. And I want to tell you: the most powerful people on earth are NOTHING compared to the Stone that is coming. Daniel 2:44 says, "The God of heaven will set up a kingdom which shall never be destroyed; and the kingdom shall not be left to other people; it shall break in pieces and consume all these kingdoms, and it shall stand forever."

Do you hear that? It shall STAND FOREVER. The world's politicians argue about who will lead the next generation. God has already decided who will lead FOREVER. His name is Jesus, and He is the Stone cut without hands. And when He strikes, every rival kingdom crumbles to powder.

Let the nations rage. Let the rulers take counsel together. Psalm 2 says that the Lord LAUGHS at them. He has already installed His King on Zion. The question is not WHETHER Christ will reign. The question is whether YOU will be part of His kingdom when He does.

---

### Zechariah 14:1-9 — The Lord Returns to the Mount of Olives

"And in that day His feet will stand on the Mount of Olives, which faces Jerusalem on the east. And the Mount of Olives shall be split in two." The SAME mountain from which Jesus ascended (Acts 1:9-12) is the mountain to which He will RETURN. His feet will touch that mountain, and it will split in half.

"And the LORD shall be King over all the earth. In that day it shall be — 'The LORD is one,' and His name one." Every rival king will be dethroned. Every false god will be exposed. Every knee will bow.

I have stood on the Mount of Olives. I have looked across the Kidron Valley at the Golden Gate — the sealed gate through which tradition says the Messiah will enter Jerusalem. And I want to tell you: that mountain is WAITING. The stones of that mountain are waiting for the feet of the King. The olive trees are waiting. The city of Jerusalem is waiting. And one day — maybe soon — those feet that were pierced with nails will touch that mountain, and it will SPLIT OPEN as if the very earth is bowing before its Creator.

The angels said it in Acts 1:11: "This same Jesus, who was taken up from you into heaven, will so come in like manner as you saw Him go into heaven." In LIKE MANNER. He went up from the Mount of Olives. He is coming back to the Mount of Olives. He went up visibly. He is coming back visibly. He went up bodily. He is coming back bodily. The same Jesus. Not a different Jesus. Not a spiritual idea. The SAME Jesus. And He is coming back.

---

### Malachi 4:1-2 — The Sun of Righteousness

"But to you who fear My name, the Sun of Righteousness shall arise with healing in His wings." The last promise in the Old Testament before 400 years of silence. A promise of the coming King who will bring healing, light, and justice.

After Malachi, God was silent for 400 years. No prophet. No revelation. Silence. And then — a baby in Bethlehem. The Sun of Righteousness had risen. And He will rise AGAIN — not as a baby, but as a blazing Sun of glory.

Four hundred years of silence. Can you imagine? Generation after generation with no word from God. The heavens were as brass. The prophets were gone. And yet — God had not forgotten His promise. He was simply WAITING. Waiting for the fullness of time. And when the time was right, He broke the silence with the cry of a newborn baby in Bethlehem.

And now we are in another season of waiting. It has been nearly 2,000 years since Jesus ascended. Some people say, "Where is the promise of His coming?" (2 Peter 3:4). They mock. They scoff. They say He is not coming. But the Bible says that God is not slow concerning His promise. He is PATIENT — not willing that any should perish, but that all should come to repentance (2 Peter 3:9). He waited 400 years between Malachi and Bethlehem, and He broke His silence spectacularly. He has been silent for 2,000 years since the ascension, and when He breaks THIS silence, it will make Bethlehem look like a whisper. Because this time, He comes not as a baby but as a KING. Not in a manger but on a white horse. Not in weakness but in POWER.

---

### Enoch — Raptured Before Judgment (Genesis 5:24, Hebrews 11:5, Jude 14-15)

"Enoch walked with God; and he was not, for God took him." Before the flood of judgment came, God took Enoch OUT. He didn't go through the flood. He was taken BEFORE it.

Jude tells us that Enoch prophesied: "Behold, the Lord comes with ten thousands of His saints, to execute judgment on all." The FIRST recorded prophecy of the Second Coming — spoken before the flood!

Enoch is a picture of the church — walking with God, then taken up before the judgment falls. 1 Thessalonians 4:17 — "We who are alive and remain shall be caught up together with them in the clouds to meet the Lord in the air."

I find it remarkable that Enoch — this man who lived before the flood, before Israel, before the law, before the prophets — was given a vision of the SECOND COMING. The very first prophecy ever recorded about the return of Christ came from a man who WALKED WITH GOD. Not a man who had theological degrees. Not a man who had a big ministry. A man who WALKED with God. Day by day. Step by step. Intimately. Faithfully.

And God rewarded that walk by taking him straight to heaven without dying. One moment walking on earth. The next moment in the presence of God. No death. No grave. No funeral. Just TAKEN.

The Bible says that is what will happen to believers who are alive when Christ returns. We will be taken. Caught up. Snatched away. One moment here. The next moment THERE. In the presence of the Lord. Just like Enoch.

And notice the contrast. Enoch was taken BEFORE the flood. Noah went THROUGH the flood. Both were saved, but Enoch was removed before the judgment came. Many Bible scholars see in this a picture of the rapture — the church removed before the great tribulation falls upon the earth. Whether you agree with every detail of that interpretation or not, one thing is clear: God knows how to deliver His people. He knows how to rescue the godly from the time of trial (2 Peter 2:9).

## B. Additional Pauline Connections

### Romans 13:11-14 — The Night Is Far Spent

"Knowing the time, that now it is high time to awake out of sleep; for now our salvation is nearer than when we first believed. The night is far spent, the day is at hand."

Paul says WAKE UP. The night of human history is almost over. The dawn of Christ's return is approaching. Every day that passes brings us one day closer. Are you ready? Are you awake?

If Paul said "the night is far spent" nearly 2,000 years ago, how much MORE is it true tonight? We are 2,000 years closer to the return of Christ than Paul was. The alarm clock is ringing. The dawn is breaking. And Paul gives us practical instructions: "Let us cast off the works of darkness, and let us put on the armor of light." Take off the old. Put on the new. Live as children of the day, not as creatures of the night.

I have preached to millions of people in my lifetime, and the message I keep coming back to is this: the time is SHORT. You do not have forever to make your decision. The night is far spent. The day is at hand. TODAY is the day of salvation. Not tomorrow. Not next week. Not when you retire. TODAY.

### 1 Corinthians 15:51-57 — The Mystery of the Rapture

"Behold, I tell you a mystery: We shall not all sleep, but we shall all be changed — in a moment, in the twinkling of an eye, at the last trumpet. For the trumpet will sound, and the dead will be raised incorruptible, and we shall be changed."

In the twinkling of an eye! Not in an hour. Not in a minute. In the TWINKLING of an eye — faster than you can blink. One moment here, the next moment with the Lord. That fast. That sudden. That glorious.

Paul calls this a "mystery." In the New Testament, a mystery is not something mysterious — it is something previously hidden that God has now REVEALED. And the mystery is this: not everyone will die. Some believers will be alive when Christ returns, and they will be CHANGED — transformed instantly — without ever tasting death. Their mortal bodies will put on immortality. Their corruptible bodies will put on incorruption.

And then Paul shouts in triumph: "O Death, where is your sting? O Hades, where is your victory?" (v. 55). Death has been DEFEATED. The grave has been CONQUERED. The King is coming, and He brings resurrection with Him. Death is not the end. It is a defeated enemy. And when the trumpet sounds, even the graves will give up their dead, and death itself will die.

### Philippians 3:20-21 — Our Citizenship Is in Heaven

"Our citizenship is in heaven, from which we also eagerly wait for the Savior, the Lord Jesus Christ, who will transform our lowly body that it may be conformed to His glorious body."

You are a citizen of heaven living temporarily on earth. And when the King returns, He will transform your broken, aging, dying body into a body like HIS — glorious, powerful, immortal. No more pain. No more sickness. No more death. A body fit for eternity.

Paul wrote this letter from a Roman prison. He was a Roman citizen, and he knew what citizenship meant — rights, privileges, protection, identity. And he said to the believers in Philippi: your REAL citizenship is not Roman. It is not Greek. It is not American or British or Chinese. Your real citizenship is in HEAVEN. This world is not your home. You are an ambassador, a representative, a pilgrim passing through. And your King is coming to take you HOME.

When He does, He will give you a new body. Not a patched-up version of this one. A GLORIFIED body. The Bible says it will be like HIS glorious body — the body that walked through walls, that ate fish on the beach, that ascended into heaven. A real body. A physical body. But a body free from every limitation, every disease, every weakness that plagues you now.

### 1 Thessalonians 4:13-18 — The Lord's Own Coming

"The Lord Himself will descend from heaven with a shout, with the voice of an archangel, and with the trumpet of God. And the dead in Christ will rise first. Then we who are alive and remain shall be caught up together with them in the clouds to meet the Lord in the air. And thus we shall always be with the Lord."

The Lord HIMSELF. Not an angel. Not a representative. The Lord HIMSELF is coming. With a SHOUT. With a TRUMPET. And the dead in Christ rise FIRST. Then we who are alive are caught up. And we shall ALWAYS be with the Lord. Always. Forever. No separation. No ending.

"Comfort one another with these words." This is not theology to argue about. It is COMFORT to live by.

I have stood beside more graves than I can count. I have held the hands of widows. I have wept with parents who lost children. And in those darkest moments, I go back to this passage. I go back to four words: "Always with the Lord." The separation is temporary. The reunion is permanent. The grave is not the end of the story. It is a PAUSE. And when the trumpet sounds, the pause is over, and the greatest reunion in history begins.

The Bible says the dead in Christ rise FIRST. If you have a loved one who died in the Lord — a mother, a father, a child, a spouse, a friend — they will rise FIRST. Before you are caught up, THEY will rise. You will see them again. Not as ghosts. Not as memories. As REAL, RESURRECTED, GLORIFIED people. Alive. Whole. Beautiful. Forever.

### 2 Thessalonians 1:7-10 — When the Lord Is Revealed

"When the Lord Jesus is revealed from heaven with His mighty angels, in flaming fire taking vengeance on those who do not know God, and on those who do not obey the gospel... when He comes, in that Day, to be glorified in His saints and to be admired among all those who believe."

The Second Coming has two sides: GLORY for those who believe, VENGEANCE for those who reject. The same event, two opposite experiences. Your relationship with Jesus determines which side you're on.

This is the most solemn truth I can tell you tonight. The return of Christ will be the BEST day in history for those who know Him, and the WORST day in history for those who don't. The same sun that melts wax hardens clay. The same event — Christ appearing in glory — will bring unspeakable joy to the believer and unspeakable terror to the unbeliever.

Which will it be for you? When you see Him coming in the clouds — and you WILL see Him, because every eye shall see Him — will your heart leap with joy, or will you cry out for the rocks and mountains to fall on you and hide you from His face (Revelation 6:16)?

The choice is yours. And the time to make that choice is NOW. Not when He appears. Now. Tonight. Before the trumpet sounds.

## C. Practical Application: Living in Light of His Return

### How the Second Coming Should Change Your Life Today

1. **Live holy** — 1 John 3:2-3: "When He is revealed, we shall be like Him... And everyone who has this hope in Him purifies himself, just as He is pure." If you really believe Jesus is coming back, it changes how you live. You clean up your life. Not out of fear, but out of LOVE. You want to be ready. You want Him to be proud of what He finds when He arrives.

2. **Stay watchful** — Matthew 24:42: "Watch therefore, for you do not know what hour your Lord is coming." Don't fall asleep spiritually. Stay alert. Stay ready. The world will try to lull you to sleep with entertainment, with comfort, with distraction. Stay AWAKE. Keep your eyes on the horizon. The King is coming.

3. **Work urgently** — John 9:4: "I must work the works of Him who sent Me while it is day; the night is coming when no one can work." There are people to reach, neighbors to love, gospel to share. The time is short. Every person you meet today is someone who will either rejoice or weep at the return of Christ. What are you doing to reach them?

4. **Comfort the grieving** — 1 Thessalonians 4:18: "Comfort one another with these words." When you stand at the grave of a believer, you don't grieve without hope. The resurrection is coming. The reunion is certain. Death has lost its sting. The grave has lost its victory. Comfort one another.

5. **Hold loosely to this world** — Colossians 3:2: "Set your mind on things above, not on things on the earth." This world is temporary. Everything you can see is passing away. Your house, your car, your bank account, your career — it is all temporary. Invest in what lasts forever. Invest in souls. Invest in the kingdom. Invest in eternity.

6. **Love one another deeply** — 1 Peter 4:7-8: "The end of all things is at hand; therefore be serious and watchful in your prayers. And above all things have fervent love for one another." When you know the end is near, what matters most? Not your possessions. Not your reputation. LOVE. Fervent, deep, sacrificial love for one another. That is what will matter when the King returns.

### Signs of the Times

- **Israel restored as a nation (1948)** — Matthew 24:32-34, the fig tree generation. For nearly 2,000 years Israel was scattered among the nations. Then, in a single day — May 14, 1948 — Israel became a nation again. The fig tree is putting forth its leaves. Jesus said, "When you see all these things, know that it is near — at the doors!" (Matthew 24:33).

- **The gospel preached worldwide** — Matthew 24:14: "And this gospel of the kingdom will be preached in all the world as a witness to all the nations, and then the end will come." Through radio, television, the internet, and missionary work, the gospel is reaching the farthest corners of the earth. We are closer to the fulfillment of this prophecy than any generation before us.

- **Increasing lawlessness** — Matthew 24:12: "Because lawlessness will abound, the love of many will grow cold." Look at the headlines. Crime. Corruption. Violence. And worse than the lawlessness itself is its effect — it causes love to grow cold. People stop caring. People stop trusting. Hearts harden. That is a sign.

- **Wars and rumors of wars** — Matthew 24:6-7. Nation against nation. Kingdom against kingdom. The 20th century saw more war dead than all previous centuries combined. And the 21st century shows no sign of slowing down.

- **Technology enabling global surveillance** — Revelation 13:16-17. A generation ago, the idea that every person on earth could be monitored, tracked, and required to have a "mark" to buy or sell seemed like science fiction. Today, it is almost science fact.

- **A turning away from the faith** — 2 Thessalonians 2:3, the falling away. In many parts of the Western world, churches are closing. Faith is declining. People are turning away from the truth. Paul warned that this would happen before the return of Christ.

These signs do not give us a DATE. The Bible says no one knows the day or the hour (Matthew 24:36). But they give us a SEASON. They tell us that we are living in the season of His return. The signs are all around us. The stage is being set. The King is coming.

## D. Broader Revelation Connections

### Revelation 1:7 — Every Eye Will See Him

"Behold, He is coming with clouds, and every eye will see Him, even they who pierced Him. And all the tribes of the earth will mourn because of Him." Not a secret return. Not a quiet return. EVERY EYE. Every person on earth will see the King of Kings descending in glory.

Think about that. When Jesus came the first time, only a handful of people knew — some shepherds, some wise men, an old man named Simeon, an old woman named Anna. He came quietly. Secretly, almost. Born in a stable. Laid in a feeding trough. The world didn't notice.

But when He comes the SECOND time, every eye will see Him. Every. Single. Eye. The president will see Him. The homeless man will see Him. The atheist will see Him. The terrorist will see Him. The child will see Him. There will be no missing it. No denying it. No explaining it away. The sky will open, and the King will appear, and every human being on the face of the earth will KNOW that Jesus Christ is Lord.

---

### Revelation 19:11-16 — The King of Kings

"Now I saw heaven opened, and behold, a white horse. And He who sat on it was called Faithful and True... His eyes were like a flame of fire, and on His head were many crowns... He was clothed with a robe dipped in blood, and His name is called The Word of God... And He has on His robe and on His thigh a name written: KING OF KINGS AND LORD OF LORDS."

The first time He came on a donkey. The next time, a white horse. The first time, a crown of thorns. The next time, MANY crowns. The first time, a robe stripped off in mockery. The next time, a robe dipped in the blood of victory. The first time they wrote "King of the Jews" as a joke. The next time, KING OF KINGS AND LORD OF LORDS — and no one will be laughing.

I want you to close your eyes for a moment and PICTURE this. Heaven splits open like a curtain torn in two. And there He is — on a white horse, blazing with glory, His eyes like fire, crowns upon His head. Behind Him, the armies of heaven — angels and saints — riding white horses, dressed in pure white linen. And on His robe and on His thigh, written in letters that cannot be erased: KING OF KINGS AND LORD OF LORDS.

This is not gentle Jesus meek and mild. This is the WARRIOR KING. This is the LION of the tribe of Judah. This is the One who holds the keys of Death and Hades. This is the Alpha and the Omega, the Beginning and the End. This is the One before whom every knee will bow and every tongue will confess that He is LORD.

And His name is "Faithful and True." He PROMISED He would return, and He is FAITHFUL. His Word is TRUE. Every promise He ever made will be fulfilled. Every prophecy will be completed. He does not lie. He does not delay beyond His appointed time. He is coming. And when He comes, He comes as KING.

---

### Revelation 20:1-6 — The Millennial Kingdom

Satan bound for 1,000 years. Christ reigning on earth. The saints reigning with Him. This is the kingdom that Daniel saw — the Stone that fills the whole earth. This is what every believer has been praying for: "Thy kingdom come, Thy will be done, on earth as it is in heaven." It IS coming.

For a thousand years, the earth will experience what it was MEANT to experience — the rule of its rightful King. No war. No injustice. No corruption. No oppression. The knowledge of the Lord will cover the earth as the waters cover the sea (Isaiah 11:9). The wolf will lie down with the lamb (Isaiah 11:6). Swords will be beaten into plowshares (Isaiah 2:4). The curse will be lifted. Creation will be restored. And Jesus Christ will reign from Jerusalem over the whole earth.

And the Bible says the saints will reign WITH Him. That means YOU, if you are a believer. You will not just be a spectator in the kingdom. You will be a PARTICIPANT. A ruler. A co-regent with the King of Kings. The faithful servant will hear, "Well done, good and faithful servant... Enter into the joy of your lord" (Matthew 25:21).

---

### Revelation 21-22 — The Eternal State

"God will wipe away every tear from their eyes; there shall be no more death, nor sorrow, nor crying. There shall be no more pain, for the former things have passed away." (Revelation 21:4)

A new heaven and a new earth. The holy city, New Jerusalem, coming down from God. The river of life. The tree of life. No more curse. No more night. God dwelling with His people FOREVER.

"And they shall reign forever and ever." Not just for a millennium. FOREVER. The King is coming back, and His kingdom has no end.

I have been asked thousands of times, "Billy, what will heaven be like?" And I always come back to Revelation 21 and 22. No more tears. No more death. No more sorrow. No more crying. No more pain. Can you even IMAGINE a world without those things? A world where no child goes hungry? Where no parent buries a son or daughter? Where no widow weeps at a graveside? Where no hospital is needed because there is no sickness? Where no lock is needed because there is no crime? Where no army is needed because there is no war?

THAT is what is coming. That is the future that belongs to every born-again believer. Not a fairy tale. Not wishful thinking. A PROMISE from the God who cannot lie, sealed by the blood of Jesus Christ, guaranteed by the resurrection from the dead.

The Bible says in Revelation 22:20, "He who testifies to these things says, 'Surely I am coming quickly.' Amen. Even so, come, Lord Jesus!"

That is the last promise of the Bible. "Surely I am coming quickly." And the response of every believer, in every generation, in every language, is the same: "Even so, come, Lord Jesus!"

Is that the cry of YOUR heart tonight? Are you ready for the King to come? If you are not sure — if there is any doubt in your mind — I urge you, come to Christ tonight. Confess your sin. Receive His forgiveness. Be born again. And then you can look up and say with confidence: "Even so, come, Lord Jesus! I am ready."

The King is coming. Are you ready?
