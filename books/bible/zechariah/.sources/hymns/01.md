> **Come, Sinners, to the Gospel Feast** \
> Charles Wesley; text from Wikisource, "Come, Sinners, to the Gospel Feast (version 1)", stanzas 3 and 9（該頁未註明印本；原擬用的 "Come, Ye Sinners, Poor and Needy" 在 Wikisource 查無可核對的文本，故改用此詩）
>
> Come, all ye souls by sin oppressed, \
> Ye restless wanderers after rest; \
> Ye poor, and maimed, and sick, and blind, \
> In Christ a hearty welcome find.
>
> This is the time, no more delay! \
> This is the Lord's accepted day. \
> Come thou, this moment, at His call, \
> And live for Him Who died for all.
>
> 來吧，所有被罪壓傷的人， \
> 你們這些尋求安息、四處飄泊的人； \
> 你們貧窮的、殘廢的、有病的、瞎眼的， \
> 在基督那裏必得誠懇的歡迎。
>
> 就是此時，不要再遲延！ \
> 這是主悅納人的日子。 \
> 就在此刻，應祂的呼召前來， \
> 為那替眾人死的主而活。
