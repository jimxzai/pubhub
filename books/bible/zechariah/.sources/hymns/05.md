> **Arise, My Soul, Arise** \
> Charles Wesley; text from *The Army and Navy Hymnal* (1920), no. 82, stanzas 1-2（為歌唱而重複的一行已略）
>
> Arise, my soul, arise! \
> Shake off thy guilty fears; \
> The bleeding Sacrifice \
> In my behalf appears; \
> Before the throne my Surety stands; \
> My name is written on his hands.
>
> He ever lives above, \
> For me to intercede, \
> His all redeeming love, \
> His precious blood to plead; \
> His blood atoned for all our race, \
> And sprinkled now the throne of grace.
>
> 我的心哪，起來，起來！ \
> 抖落你有罪的恐懼； \
> 那流血的祭物 \
> 為我顯現； \
> 我的中保站在寶座前， \
> 我的名字寫在祂的手上。
>
> 祂永遠活在高天， \
> 為我代求， \
> 用祂完全救贖的愛， \
> 用祂寶貴的血懇求； \
> 祂的血為全人類贖了罪， \
> 如今灑在施恩的寶座上。
