# Hymn bank for chapter 10: Trust and Obey

## 配詩 opening block (verbatim; used in 1 chapters, e.g. johannine-epistles/02-1john-ch2.md)

> **Trust and Obey**
> *John H. Sammis, 1887*
>
> When we walk with the Lord in the light of His Word,
> What a glory He sheds on our way!
> While we do His good will, He abides with us still,
> And with all who will trust and obey.
>
> Trust and obey, for there's no other way
> To be happy in Jesus, but to trust and obey.
>
> 與主同行在光明話語中，
> 何等榮光照亮前路！
> 當我遵行他美善旨意時，
> 主與信靠順服者同在。

## continuation candidate 1 (heading "Trust and Obey（詩歌延伸）", e.g. chronicl1/10-end-of-saul.md)

「信而順服，因為除此以外，別無他法能在耶穌裡得著喜樂」——這句副歌，正是對第13節「沒有遵守耶和華的命」最溫柔卻也最尖銳的回應：路只有一條，就是信而順服。
