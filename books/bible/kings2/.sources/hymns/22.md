# Hymn bank for chapter 22: Break Thou the Bread of Life

## 配詩 opening block (verbatim; used in 3 chapters, e.g. gospel-of-john-niv/06-bread-of-life.md)

> **Break Thou the Bread of Life**
> *Mary A. Lathbury, 1877*
>
> Break Thou the bread of life, dear Lord, to me,
> As Thou didst break the loaves beside the sea;
> Beyond the sacred page I seek Thee, Lord;
> My spirit pants for Thee, O Living Word!
>
> 主，為我擘開生命餅，如在海邊擘餅；
> 我要超越聖經文字，尋求你活潑話語！

## continuation candidate 1 (heading "Break Thou the Bread of Life（見上「配詩」）", e.g. thessanlonian1/04-word-at-work.md)

「越過書頁的字句，我尋求你」——帖撒羅尼迦人沒有停在保羅的話上，他們從人的口中聽出了神的道（2:13）；這首詩所求的，正是越過字句遇見那永活的道。
