# Hymn bank for chapter 19: Sweet Hour of Prayer

## 配詩 opening block (verbatim; used in 1 chapters, e.g. james/05-patience-and-prayer.md)

> **Sweet Hour of Prayer** \
> *William Walford, 1845*
>
> Sweet hour of prayer, sweet hour of prayer, \
> That calls me from a world of care, \
> And bids me at my Father's throne \
> Make all my wants and wishes known.
>
> 甜美禱告時，甜美禱告時， \
> 呼召我離開憂慮世界， \
> 吩咐我來到天父座前， \
> 傾心吐意，將所需說明。
>
> Sweet hour of prayer, sweet hour of prayer, \
> Thy wings shall my petition bear \
> To Him whose truth and faithfulness \
> Engage the waiting soul to bless.
>
> 甜美禱告時，甜美禱告時， \
> 願你的翅膀承載我祈求， \
> 飛向那信實又真實的主， \
> 祂必應允等候的心靈。

## continuation candidate 1 (heading "Sweet Hour of Prayer", e.g. gospel-of-mark/11-triumphal-entry.md)

> **Sweet hour of prayer! sweet hour of prayer!** \
> That calls me from a world of care, \
> And bids me at my Father's throne \
> Make all my wants and wishes known. \
> In seasons of distress and grief, \
> My soul has often found relief, \
> And oft escaped the tempter's snare, \
> **By thy return, sweet hour of prayer!**

殿之為殿，不在石頭與金子，在乎其中有沒有人來到父的施恩座前。禱告不是宗教活動的點綴，是殿的本質。

## continuation candidate 2 (heading "Sweet Hour of Prayer", e.g. joshua/08-gibeonite-deception.md)

這首詩歌所歌頌的禱告時刻，正是本章的首領們沒有留給自己的那一段時間。他們忙著驗餅、驗袋、驗鞋，卻沒有一刻「在天父的寶座前」把這件事說出來。
