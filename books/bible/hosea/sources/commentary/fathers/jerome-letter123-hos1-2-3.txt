13. It is true that the patriarchs had each of them more wives than one and that they had numerous concubines besides. And as if their example was not enough, David had many wives and Solomon a countless number. Judah went in to Tamar thinking her to be a harlot; Genesis 38:12-18 and according to the letter that kills the prophet Hosea married not only a whore but an adulteress. Hosea 1:2-3 If these instances are to justify us let us neigh after every woman that we meet; Jeremiah 5:8 like the people of Sodom and Gomorrha let us be found by the last day buying and selling, marrying and giving in marriage; Luke 17:27-29 and let us only end our marrying with the close of our lives. And if both before and after the deluge the maxim held good: be fruitful and multiply and replenish the earth: what has that to do with us upon whom the ends of the ages have come, unto whom it is said, the time is short, 1 Corinthians 7:29 and now the axe is laid unto the root of the trees; that is to say, the forests of marriage and of the law must be cut down by the chastity of the gospel. There is a time to embrace, and a time to refrain from embracing. Ecclesiastes 3:5 Owing to the near approach of the captivity Jeremiah is forbidden to take a wife. Jeremiah 16:2 In Babylon Ezekiel says: my wife is dead and my mouth is opened. Neither he who wished to marry nor he who had married could in wedlock prophesy freely. In days gone by men rejoiced to hear it said of them: your children shall be like olive plants round about your table, and you shall see your children's children. But now it is said of those who live in continence: he that is joined unto the Lord is one spirit; 1 Corinthians 6:17 and my soul follows hard after you: your right hand upholds me. Then it was said an eye for an eye; now the commandment is whosoever shall smite you on your right cheek, turn to him the other also. Matthew 5:38-39 In those days men said to the warrior: gird your sword upon your thigh, O most mighty; now it is said to Peter: put up again your sword into his place: for all they that take the sword shall perish with the sword.
