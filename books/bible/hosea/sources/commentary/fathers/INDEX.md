# Early-church-father comment on Hosea: source index

All files are public-domain English translations (Ante-Nicene Fathers and Nicene and Post-Nicene Fathers, series 1 and 2) fetched from newadvent.org and saved as plain text (HTML tags stripped, whitespace runs collapsed to single spaces; inline Scripture cross-references added by the site, such as "Hosea 6:6", are left in place). Each file starts with a header: AUTHOR, WORK (with the site's section heading), TRANSLATOR, SOURCE_URL, HOSEA_VERSE, CITATION (and NOTE where a very long paragraph was cut to an excerpt at sentence boundaries). The text after the header is the paragraph(s) containing the Hosea passage, copied from the downloaded page without alteration.

Method: about 3,160 newadvent.org father pages (ANF 1-9 and NPNF 1/1-1/14, 2/1-2/14 sections the site carries as chapter pages) were downloaded and searched for "Hosea", "Osee", and quoted Hosea phrases (out of Egypt, I desired mercy, O death where is thy, wife of whoredoms, many days without a king, third day, and others). Every hit was read; passing mentions of Hosea as a name or chronology marker were discarded. 103 passages are kept.

Verification (run by script, not by eye): (1) every saved passage is a verbatim substring of the downloaded page text, after whitespace normalisation; (2) every quotable below is a verbatim substring of its saved file, 60 words or fewer, and was cut by start/end phrase from the file by the script, not typed. Quotables are tagged with the Hosea verse they explain. The CITATION section number (the section sign) is the number of the nearest preceding numbered paragraph on the newadvent page; where a page has no numbered paragraphs only book/chapter is given.

Use column: I = the father applies or explains the verse in some way; C = citation only (the verse is quoted for another point, with no comment on Hosea). Both are kept so the study book can see where a verse is merely used.

Caveats the reader should know:

- Verse tags come from the site's own cross-reference links, which follow English/Hebrew numbering and are sometimes off where the father quotes the Septuagint (for example Hos 12:1, 13:2, 14:9/14:8, 13:3 vs 13:8). Where I changed or qualified a tag, the HOSEA_VERSE line says so. Jerome, Letter 130 and Basil, Letter 46 carry no site tag; the verse was identified from the quoted words and is flagged in the header.
- Several fathers quote Hosea in a Septuagint or Old Latin form that differs from the modern text (Hos 11:1 "his sons" in the LXX, Hos 4:6, 12:1, 13:2, 13:14 in Lactantius and Leo). The quotables reproduce what the father wrote, not the modern reading.
- Fathers read some verses differently from modern exegesis: Hos 6:2 "after two days ... third day" is read as Christ's resurrection (Tertullian, Lactantius, Augustine, Cyril, Rufinus, Gregory Nazianzen) or as the believer's consolation (Chrysostom, Letter to a Young Widow); Hos 13:14 as Christ's victory over death; Hos 1:2-3 as Christ and the Church (Irenaeus) or Gentiles called (Origen, Augustine). These are recorded as the fathers say them.
- Pseudo-Hippolytus, On the End of the World, and the Apostolic Constitutions are not by the authors the names suggest; they are labelled as the site and ANF label them.
- Jerome's Commentary on Hosea is not in NPNF and is not used. Commentaries on Hosea by Theodore of Mopsuestia, Theodoret and Cyril of Alexandria are likewise not in this corpus. All material below is therefore incidental comment in treatises, letters and homilies, not running commentary on Hosea.
