## How the set was found

- gty.org has NO Hosea verse-by-verse series. Its own Scripture filter is client-rendered and unusable; the page-level Scripture tag was read from every fetched page and none carries Hosea as its listed Scripture.
- So the whole site was swept: `sitemap.xml` (fetched with curl and a full browser User-Agent) lists 4,432 sermon and article URLs (4,429 distinct pages after three duplicate entries); all were fetched (zero failures), stored in the session scratchpad cache, and every transcript (`div.expand--content`) was searched for `Hosea` / `Hos.`; 3,573 pages carry a transcript container, the rest are video-only or article pages (article pages were searched as whole-page text). Further phrase searches (Gomer, Lo-ammi, Jezreel, Lo-ruhamah, "fallow ground", "sow the wind", "How can I give you up", "Valley of Achor", "door of hope") caught uses where the book is not named. 283 transcripts contain "Hosea" or "Hos." (many are only the book list "Hosea, Joel, Amos..."; "Hosea" also appears as the name of a king in an Isaiah sermon, 1383). The Gomer phrase search was read in context, because Gomer is also a son of Japheth (Gen 10) and the name sits inside "Montgomery".
- Selection rule: kept a file only where a Hosea passage is a primary text, or is read and explained over several consecutive paragraphs. BRIEF = 1-3 paragraphs. Passing mentions are not downloaded (list at the bottom).
- Weight words: PRIMARY = Hosea is the point of a stretch of the sermon, verses read or retold in order; SUBSTANTIAL SECTION = 4-6 paragraphs inside a sermon on another text; BRIEF = 1-3 paragraphs.

## Honest scope note

MacArthur has no Hosea series on gty.org: there is no sermon with Hosea as its listed Scripture. What exists is (a) the Gomer story retold as an illustration in marriage/divorce sermons (2219, 2337, 1212, 90-230, 41-49) and in Matt 2 Christmas sermons that also explain Hos 11:1; (b) Hosea quoted where the NT quotes him (Rom 9:25-26, 1 Pet 2:10, Matt 9:13, Luke 23:30, Heb 13:15); (c) a few paragraphs on single verses. **Units with NO usable MacArthur material: 8 (Hos 7), 13 (Hos 11:12-12:14), and, apart from a single sentence each, 6 (Hos 5) and 10 (Hos 9).** Units 5 and 9 have only BRIEF paragraphs. Units 7 and 11 have one SUBSTANTIAL SECTION each, on 6:6 and 10:8 only. Every use of the Gomer material is an application sermon, not an exposition of the verses; the book should not present these as MacArthur commentary on Hosea 1-3 beyond what the quotables say. 80-163, 2185 and 81-7 are three deliveries of closely overlapping Christmas material (same Hos 11:1 and Hos 1-3 summary) and should not be counted as three independent witnesses; 2219, 2337, 1212 and 90-230 / 41-49 are the same marriage-sermon material across decades.
