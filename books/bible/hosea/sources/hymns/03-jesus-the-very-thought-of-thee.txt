TITLE: Jesus, the Very Thought of Thee
AUTHOR: Bernard of Clairvaux (attributed), tr. Edward Caswall (per source page)
YEAR: not stated on source page (text below from Army and Navy Hymnal 1920)
SOURCE_URL: https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/The_Army_and_Navy_Hymnal/Hymns/Jesus,_the_Very_Thought_of_Thee

O hope of every contrite heart,
O Joy of all the meek,
To those who fall, how kind thou art!
How kind to those who seek!

But what to those who find? Ah, this
Nor tongue nor pen could show;
The love of Jesus, what it is
None but his loved ones know.
