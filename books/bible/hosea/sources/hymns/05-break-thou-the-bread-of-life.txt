TITLE: Break Thou the Bread of Life
AUTHOR: Mary Artemisia Lathbury
YEAR: 1877 (first two verses, per source page note; text below from Army and Navy Hymnal 1920)
SOURCE_URL: https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/The_Army_and_Navy_Hymnal/Hymns/Break_Thou_the_Bread_of_Life

Break thou the bread of life, Dear lord to me,
As thou didst break the loaves Beside the sea;
Beyond the sacred page I seek thee, Lord;
My spirit pants for thee, O living Word!

Bless thou the truth, dear Lord, To me, to me,
As thou didst bless the bread by Galilee;
Then shall all bondage cease, All fetters fall;
And I shall find my peace, My All-in-All.
