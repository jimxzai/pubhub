TITLE: Dies Irae ("Day of wrath! O day of mourning!")
AUTHOR: Thomas of Celano (Tommaso da Celano), tr. William Josiah Irons
YEAR: 1849 (translation date as stated on source page; page text from the 1912 English Missal)
SOURCE_URL: https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Dies_Irae_(Irons,_1912)

Lo! The book, exactly worded,
wherein all hath been recorded:
thence shall judgment be awarded.

When the Judge his seat attaineth,
and each hidden deed arraigneth,
nothing unavenged remaineth.
