TITLE: I Will Sing the Wondrous Story
AUTHOR: Francis Harold Rowley (per source page)
YEAR: not stated on source page (text below from Army and Navy Hymnal 1920)
SOURCE_URL: https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/The_Army_and_Navy_Hymnal/Hymns/I_Will_Sing_the_Wondrous_Story

I was lost, but Jesus found me, Found the sheep that went astray;
Threw his loving arms around me, Drew me back into his way.

I was bruised, but Jesus healed me, Faint was I from many a fall,
Sight was gone and fears possessed me, But he freed me from them all.
