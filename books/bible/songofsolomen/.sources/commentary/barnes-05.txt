# Barnes' Notes on the Bible on Song of Solomon (unit 05); source: biblehub.com/commentaries/songs/<chapter>-<verse>.htm (public domain; fetched 2026-10-26). Sections repeated verbatim on several verse pages kept once, at first verse.

===== [Song of Solomon 3:1] =====
By night - i. e., In the night-hours.

===== [Song of Solomon 3:3] =====
The city - One near the bride's native home, possibly Shunem.

===== [Song of Solomon 3:4] =====
I held him - This begins the fourth stanza. The bride's mother is mentioned again in Sol 6:9; Sol 8:2.
