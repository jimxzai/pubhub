# Geneva Study Bible on Song of Solomon (unit 01); source: biblehub.com/commentaries/songs/<chapter>-<verse>.htm (public domain; fetched 2026-10-26). Sections repeated verbatim on several verse pages kept once, at first verse.

===== [Song of Solomon 1:1] =====
The song of songs, which is Solomon's.

===== [Song of Solomon 1:2] =====
Let {a} him kiss me with the kisses of his mouth: for thy love is better than wine.(a) This is spoken in the person of the Church, or of the faithful soul inflamed with the desire of Christ, whom she loves.

===== [Song of Solomon 1:3] =====
Because of the {b} savour of thy good ointments thy name is as ointment poured forth, therefore do the {c} virgins love thee.(b) The feeling of your great benefits.(c) They that are pure in heart and conversation.

===== [Song of Solomon 1:4] =====
{d} Draw me, we will run after thee: the king hath brought me into his {e} chambers: we will be glad and rejoice in thee, we will remember thy love more than wine: the upright love thee.(d) The faithful confess that they cannot come to Christ, unless they are drawn.(e) Meaning the secret joy that is not known to the world.
