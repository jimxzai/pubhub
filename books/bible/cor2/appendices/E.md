# Appendix E: A Reading Plan for 2 Corinthians 哥林多後書讀經計劃

## 十三日讀經計劃 A Thirteen-Day Plan (一天一章 One Chapter a Day)

| **天 Day** | **經文 Passage** | **焦點 Focus** |
|:---|:---|:---|
| 1 | 1:1-24 | 患難中的安慰、誠實的事奉 *Comfort in Affliction, Sincerity of Ministry* |
| 2 | 2:1-17 | 饒恕修復、基督馨香之氣 *Forgiveness, the Aroma of Christ* |
| 3 | 3:1-18 | 新約執事、榮上加榮 *Ministers of the New Covenant, Glory to Glory* |
| 4 | 4:1-18 | 光照人心、瓦器中的寶貝、永恆眼光 *The Light of the Gospel, Jars of Clay, Eternal Perspective* |
| 5 | 5:1-21 | 天家盼望、和好的職分 *Our Heavenly Dwelling, the Ministry of Reconciliation* |
| 6 | 6:1-18 | 分別為聖、永生神的殿 *Set Apart, the Temple of the Living God* |
| 7 | 7:1-16 | 敞開心門、依神的意思憂愁 *Open Hearts, Godly Sorrow* |
| 8 | 8:1-24 | 恩典的奉獻、忠心的管理 *The Grace of Giving, Faithful Stewardship* |
| 9 | 9:1-15 | 甘心樂意、感恩榮神 *The Cheerful Giver, Thanksgiving and Glory to God* |
| 10 | 10:1-18 | 屬靈爭戰、真實的權柄 *Spiritual Warfare, True Authority* |
| 11 | 11:1-33 | 純正的福音、以軟弱誇口 *The Pure Gospel, Boasting in Weakness* |
| 12 | 12:1-21 | 身上的一根刺、恩典夠用 *The Thorn in the Flesh, Sufficient Grace* |
| 13 | 13:1-14 | 自我省察、三一的祝福 *Self-Examination, the Trinitarian Blessing* |

## 四週讀經計劃 A Compressed Four-Week Plan (小組查經適用 For Group Study)

若時間有限，可依循原24課主日學進度，每主日一課，約六個月讀畢全書；亦可壓縮為四週查經，大致對應全書兩部：\
*For those following the original 24-lesson Sunday School pace, one lesson per Sunday covers the whole book in about six months. Alternatively, the thirteen chapters may be compressed into a four-week group study, roughly following the book’s two parts:*

| **週 Week** | **經文 Passage** | **主題 Theme** |
|:---|:---|:---|
| 第一週 | 1-3章 | 患難中的安慰與新約執事的憑據 *Comfort in Affliction, the Credentials of a New Covenant Minister* |
| 第二週 | 4-7章 | 瓦器中的能力、天家盼望、和好與分別為聖 *Power in Jars of Clay, Heavenly Hope, Reconciliation and Holiness* |
| 第三週 | 8-10章 | 恩典的奉獻與屬靈爭戰 *The Grace of Giving, Spiritual Warfare* |
| 第四週 | 11-13章 | 護衛純正福音、身上的刺、愛中的權柄 *Guarding the Pure Gospel, the Thorn, Authority in Love* |
