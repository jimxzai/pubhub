TITLE: Awake, Jerusalem, Awake!
AUTHOR: Charles Wesley
YEAR: not stated on source page (page tagged PD-old)
SOURCE_URL: https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Awake,_Jerusalem,_Awake!

Awake, Jerusalem, awake!
No longer in thy sins lie down;
The garment of salvation take,
Thy beauty and thy strength put on.

Shake off the dust that blinds thy sight,
And hides the promise from thine eyes;
Arise, and struggle into light,
The great Deliverer calls: Arise!
