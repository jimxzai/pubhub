TITLE: He Comes! He Comes! the Judge Severe!
AUTHOR: Charles Wesley
YEAR: not stated on source page (page tagged PD-old)
SOURCE_URL: https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/He_Comes!_He_Comes!_the_Judge_Severe!

Descending on His great white throne,
He claims the kingdoms for His own;
The kingdoms all obey His Word,
And hail Him their triumphant Lord.

Shout, all the people of the sky,
And all the saints of the Most High;
Our Lord, Who now His right obtains,
Forever and forever reigns.
