TITLE: There Is a Fountain (Praise for the Fountain Opened)
AUTHOR: William Cowper (1731-1800, per source page)
YEAR: 1772 (first publication, per en.wikisource page https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Praise_for_the_Fountain_Opened ; text below from Army and Navy Hymnal 1920)
SOURCE_URL: https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/The_Army_and_Navy_Hymnal/Hymns/There_Is_a_Fountain

There is a fountain filled with blood,
Drawn from Immanuel's veins;
And sinners plunged beneath that flood,
Lose all their guilty stains,
Lose all their guilty stains.

The dying thief rejoiced to see
That fountain in his day;
And there may I, though vile as he,
Wash all my sins away,
Wash all my sins away.
