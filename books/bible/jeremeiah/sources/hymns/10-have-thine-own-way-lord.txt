TITLE: Have Thine Own Way, Lord
AUTHOR: Adelaide A. Pollard
YEAR: 1906 (author date) / published 1907, per hymnary.org page, which lists Copyright: Public Domain; text is the page representative text (United Methodist Hymnal 1989 printing)
SOURCE_URL: https://hymnary.org/text/have_thine_own_way_lord

Have thine own way, Lord!
Have thine own way!
Thou art the potter,
I am the clay.
Mold me and make me
after thy will,
while I am waiting,
yielded and still.

Have thine own way, Lord!
Have thine own way!
Search me and try me,
Savior today!
Wash me just now, Lord,
wash me just now,
as in thy presence
humbly I bow.
