TITLE: God Will Take Care of You (Be Not Dismayed Whate'er Betide)
AUTHOR: Civilla Durfee Martin
YEAR: 1905 (as stated on source page: "Published 1905")
SOURCE_URL: https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/The_Canadian_Soldiers%27_Song_Book/God_Will_Take_Care_of_You

Be not dismayed whate'er betide,
 God will take care of you!
Beneath His wings of love abide,
 God will take care of you!
Chorus
God will take care of you,
 Through every day, o'er all the way;
He will take care of you;
 God will take care of you!

Through days of toil when heart doth fail,
 God will take care of you!
When dangers fierce your path assail,
 God will take care of you!
