TITLE: O Love That Will Not Let Me Go
AUTHOR: George Matheson
YEAR: 1882 (as stated on source page)
SOURCE_URL: https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/The_Army_and_Navy_Hymnal/Hymns/O_Love_That_Will_Not_Let_Me_Go

O Love that will not let me go
I rest my weary soul in thee;
I give thee back the life I owe,
That in thine ocean depths its flow
May richer, fuller be.

O Light that followest all my way,
I Yield my flickering torch to thee;
My heart restores it borrowed ray,
That in , thy sun-shine's blaze its day
May brighter, fairer be.
