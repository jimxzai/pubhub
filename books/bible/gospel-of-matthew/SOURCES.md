## Citation rule

Use a source ID plus page or stable URL in the chapter text. A source note such as “整合三方資源” is not sufficient for publication. Direct quotations must remain in quotation marks; close paraphrases must be labeled as paraphrases.

## Minimum record

For each source, retain: author, full title, translator/editor, publisher, publication year, edition, page range, URL/access date where applicable, rights holder, permission status, and the chapters that use it.
