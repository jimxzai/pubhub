Veni Creator Spiritus ("Come Creator Spirit") is a hymn believed to have been written by Rabanus Maurus in the 9th century.

1733083Veni Creator Spiritus (Come Creator Spirit) — 9th centuryRabanus Maurus

English-language translations of Veni Creator Spiritus include:

Stone carving at Guilford Cathedral, UK
Come, Holy Ghost, Creator Blest by Richard Mant

Come, Holy Ghost, our souls inspire, by John Cosin

Come, O Creator Spirit blest, by Edward Caswall

Come, O Creator Spirit, come, by Robert Bridges

Creator Spirit, by whose aid, by John Dryden

Come, Holy Ghost, Creator, come, anonymous translation

"Veni Creator Spiritus" in The Little Book of the Most Holy Child Jesus by John Priestly Warmoll, 1876.
