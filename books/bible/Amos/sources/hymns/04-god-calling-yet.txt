TITLE: God Calling Yet (SUBSTITUTE for 'Sinners, Turn: Why Will Ye Die?', which is not on Wikisource as full text)
AUTHOR: Gerhard Tersteegen, tr. Jane Laurie Borthwick and Sarah Laurie Borthwick Findlater (as stated on source page)
YEAR: not stated on source page (The Army and Navy Hymnal, 1920)
SOURCE_URL: https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/The_Army_and_Navy_Hymnal/Hymns/God_Calling_Yet

God calling yet! shall I not hear?
Earth's pleasures shall I still hold dear?
Shall life's swift passing years all fly,
And still my soul in slumber lie?

God calling yet! shall I not rise?
Can I his loving voice despise,
And basely his kind care repay?
He calls me still; can I delay?

God call-ing yet! and shall he knock,
And I my heart the closer lock?
He still is waiting to receive,
And shall I dare his Spirit grieve?

God calling yet! I cannot stay;
My heart I yield without despise,
Vain world, farewell! from thee I part;
The voice of God hath reached my heart.
