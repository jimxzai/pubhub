## Substitutions
- 04 and 05: "Sinners, Turn: Why Will Ye Die?" and "Come, Ye Sinners, Poor and Needy" exist on Wikisource only as table-of-contents entries and commentary, not as lyrics. hymnary.org (403), hymntime.com and cyberhymnal.org (unreachable / 403) were blocked. Replaced by two call-to-repentance hymns that are on Wikisource.
- 12: "Jesus Shall Reign Where'er the Sun" is on Wikisource (Army and Navy Hymnal, djvu/176) but the lines are OCR-scrambled out of order, so it was not stored. Replaced by a missions hymn of the same theme.
