TITLE: Arise, My Soul, Arise
AUTHOR: Charles Wesley (1708-1788, per source page)
YEAR: not stated on source page (1742 per en.wikisource versions page https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Arise,_My_Soul,_Arise_(Wesley,_1742) ; text below from The Army and Navy Hymnal, 1920)
SOURCE_URL: https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/The_Army_and_Navy_Hymnal/Hymns/Arise,_My_Soul,_Arise

Arise, my soul, arise!
Shake off thy guilty fears;
The bleeding Sacrifice
In my behalf appears;
Before the throne my Surety stands,
Before the throne my Surety stands;
My name is written on his hands.

He ever lives above,
For me to intercede,
His all redeeming love,
His precious blood to plead;
His blood atoned for all our race,
And sprinkled now the throne of grace.

My God is reconciled ;
His pardoning voice I hear;
He owns me for his child;
I can no longer fear;
With confidence I now draw nigh,
With confidence I now draw nigh,
And Father, Abba, Father, cry.
