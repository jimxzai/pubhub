# Mark: remaining release evidence

This is an editorial working edition, not an authorization to distribute.
Technical repair does not establish the right to reproduce a work, and an
automated accessibility pass is not a manual assistive-technology review.

## Permissions owner

Locate existing signed permissions first. For NASB 1995, record permission for
the complete Gospel of Mark, surrounding quotations, bilingual commentary,
PDF/EPUB/print formats, territories, circulation, price, and license term.
Lockman's published default allowance excludes a complete Bible book; being
below 1,000 verses does not by itself clear this edition. See the official
[quotation policy](https://www.lockman.org/permission-to-quote-copyright-trademark-information/)
and [request form](https://www.lockman.org/permission-to-quote-request-form/).
No request has been submitted on the user's behalf.

Use manuscript Appendix A as the starting source inventory, not as proof of
permission. For each third-party excerpt retain: manuscript location, source
edition/translator, source page or stable URL, original wording, translation
status, amount reproduced, applicable rights basis, permission document and
reviewer/date. Include opening hymns, closing hymns, MacArthur book extracts,
sermon transcripts, Morgan's Mark and John works, and older commentators'
specific translations. Do not assume every old text needs a new license, or
that its modern translation is automatically public domain.

## Independent proofreader

Recheck every Scripture verse against the stated CUV 1919/NASB 1995 sources.
Record omissions, brackets, punctuation and attribution separately from
interpretive commentary. Verify the historical claims of 235 checked quotations
against retained evidence before confirming that count. Review Mark 16's textual
note and red-letter attribution. Sign off against the final PDF/EPUB hashes.

## Accessibility reviewer

Retain EPUBCheck and Ace reports for the exact final EPUB. Manually test a
bilingual screen-reader session, keyboard navigation, navigation depth, reading
order, verse-number pronunciation, mixed English/Chinese/Greek passages and
complex tables. The PDF remains untagged. Do not advertise PDF/UA or EPUB/WCAG
conformance before the applicable review is complete.

## Print production

The PDF uses 7-by-10-inch pages with matching front and back cover designs.
These pages are not a printer-specific wraparound cover: a spine, bleed,
binding allowance, barcode and color/output profile require the printer's
specification and final distribution metadata. No ISBN or permission evidence
has been invented to fill those gaps.
