HYMN: Holy, Holy, Holy
AUTHOR: Reginald Heber, 1826
STANZA: 1 of 4
---
Holy, holy, holy! Lord God Almighty!
Early in the morning
Our song shall rise to Thee;
Holy, holy, holy, merciful and mighty!
God in three Persons, blessèd Trinity!
