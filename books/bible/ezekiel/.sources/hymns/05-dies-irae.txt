HYMN: Dies Iræ (Irons translation)
AUTHOR: Tommaso da Celano, tr. William Josiah Irons, 1849
STANZA: 1 of 18
---
DAY of Wrath! O Day of mourning!
See! once more the Cross returning,
Heav'n and earth in ashes burning!
