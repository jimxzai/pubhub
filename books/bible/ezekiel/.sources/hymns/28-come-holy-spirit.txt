HYMN: Come, Holy Spirit, Heavenly Dove
AUTHOR: Isaac Watts, 1709 (Cyber; list said 1707)
STANZA: 1 of 5
---
Come, Holy Spirit, heav’nly Dove,
With all Thy quick’ning powers;
Kindle a flame of sacred love
In these cold hearts of ours.
