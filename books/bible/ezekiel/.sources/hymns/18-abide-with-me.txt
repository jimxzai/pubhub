HYMN: Abide with Me (Lyte)
AUTHOR: Henry Lyte, 1847
STANZA: 1 of 8
---
Abide with me!
Fast falls the eventide;
The darkness thickens:
Lord, with me abide.
When other helpers fail,
And comforts flee,
Help of the helpless,
O abide with me!
