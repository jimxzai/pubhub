EZEKIEL: 1:7-20 (cherubim, wheels)
---
The New Testament I will briefly deal with. Matthew, Mark, Luke and John are the Lord's team of four, the true cherubim or store of knowledge. With them the whole body is full of eyes, they glitter as sparks, Ezekiel 1:7 they run and return like lightning, Ezekiel 1:14 their feet are straight feet, Ezekiel 1:7 and lifted up, their backs also are winged, ready to fly in all directions. They hold together each by each and are interwoven one with another: Ezekiel 1:11 like wheels within wheels they roll along Ezekiel 1:16 and go wherever the breath of the Holy Spirit wafts them.
