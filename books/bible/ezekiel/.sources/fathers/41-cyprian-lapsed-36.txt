36. If a man make prayer with his whole heart, if he groan with the true lamentations and tears of repentance, if be incline the Lord to pardon of his sin by righteous and continual works, he who expressed His mercy in these words may pity such men: When you turn and lament, then shall you be saved, and shall know where you have been. Isaiah 30:51 And again: I have no pleasure in the death of him that dies, says the Lord, hut that he should return and live.
