EZEKIEL: 3:18
---
For it is to be feared that we shall have to hear these words concerning those who have been entrusted to us: I will require their souls at your hands; Ezekiel 3:18 and, Because you have rejected me, and not been leaders and rulers of my people, I also will reject you, that I should not be king over you; and, As you refused to hearken to My voice, and turned a stubborn back, and were disobedient, so shall it be when you call upon Me, and I will not regard nor give ear to your prayer. God forbid that these words should come to us from the just Judge, for when we sing of His mercy we must also by all means sing of His judgment.
