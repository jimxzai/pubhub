## How the set was found

- gty.org has NO Jeremiah verse-by-verse series. Its own Scripture filter (`/sermons/scripture/jeremiah`) is client-rendered and returns nothing usable; only two sermons carry `Jeremiah` as the listed Scripture (80-42 and 80-367).
- So the whole site was swept: `sitemap.xml` lists 3,928 sermon and article pages plus 1,582 blog pages; all were fetched with curl (full browser User-Agent works; no browser tool was needed) and every transcript was searched for `Jeremiah`/`Jer.` references, then candidates were read in context. Roughly 2,950 sermon pages have a transcript; the other pages are articles/blogs/video-only.
- Selection rule: kept a file only where Jeremiah is a primary text, or a passage of Jeremiah is read and explained over several consecutive paragraphs. Marked BRIEF where it is only 2-3 paragraphs. Passing mentions are not downloaded (list at the bottom).
- The Hebrews 8 series (58-xx) and Luke 22 new-covenant sermons did not turn up as Jer 31 discussions in the sitemap sweep; the Jer 31 coverage below comes from other sermons.
