## ch.1 (Nah 1:1-8) — God Moves in a Mysterious Way

> **God Moves in a Mysterious Way** \
> William Cowper; text from *Olney Hymns* (1840 ed.), Book III, Hymn 15 "Light shining out of Darkness", stanzas 1, 2
>
> God moves in a mysterious way \
> His wonders to perform; \
> He plants his footsteps in the sea, \
> And rides upon the storm.
>
> 神行事的方式奧祕難測， \
> 祂在那裏施行奇事； \
> 祂的腳蹤踏在海中， \
> 祂乘駕著風暴而行。
>
> Deep in unfathomable mines \
> Of never-failing skill, \
> He treasures up his bright designs, \
> And works his sovereign will.
>
> 在深不可測的礦藏裏， \
> 有祂永不失手的巧妙； \
> 祂把光明的籌算積存起來， \
> 成就祂至高的旨意。
>
> 中譯為編者自譯。

## ch.2 (Nah 1:9-15) — How Beauteous Are Their Feet

> **How Beauteous Are Their Feet** \
> Isaac Watts（編者依通行歸屬；該頁未署作者）; text from *A Selection of Hymns … Ulverston* (1863), Hymn 48, stanzas 1, 2
>
> How beauteous are their feet \
> Who stand on Sion's hill; \
> Who bring salvation on their tongues, \
> And words of peace reveal!
>
> 站在錫安山上的人， \
> 他們的腳何其美好； \
> 口中帶來救恩， \
> 又顯明平安的話語！
>
> How happy are our ears, \
> That hear this joyful sound, \
> Which Kings and Prophets waited for, \
> And sought, but never found!
>
> 我們的耳朵何等有福， \
> 得聽這喜樂的聲音； \
> 君王與先知曾經等候， \
> 尋求，卻終未得見！
>
> 中譯為編者自譯。

## ch.3 (Nah 2:1-13) — A Mighty Fortress Is Our God

> **A Mighty Fortress Is Our God** \
> Martin Luther, tr. Frederick H. Hedge (1853); text from *The Army and Navy Hymnal* (1920), stanzas 1, 2
>
> A mighty fortress is our God, \
> A bulwark never failing; \
> Our helper he, amid the flood \
> Of mortal ills prevailing. \
> For still our ancient foe \
> Doth seek to work us woe; \
> His craft and power are great; \
> And armed with cruel hate, \
> On earth is not his equal.
>
> 我們的神是堅固的保障， \
> 是永不傾倒的堡壘； \
> 在必死之人災禍的洪流中， \
> 祂是我們的幫助。 \
> 古時的仇敵， \
> 仍想加害於我們； \
> 他的詭計與權勢極大， \
> 又披著殘酷的恨意， \
> 地上無人能與他匹敵。
>
> Did we in our own strength confide, \
> Our striving would be losing, \
> Were not the right man on our side, \
> The man of God's own choosing. \
> Dost ask who that may be? \
> Christ Jesus, it is he, \
> Lord Sabaoth his name, \
> From age to age the same, \
> And he must win the battle.
>
> 我們若倚靠自己的力量， \
> 奮鬥終歸失敗； \
> 若不是有合宜的那一位站在我們這邊， \
> 就是神親自揀選的那一人。 \
> 你問那是誰嗎？ \
> 就是基督耶穌， \
> 萬軍之主是祂的名， \
> 歷世歷代永不改變， \
> 這場爭戰祂必得勝。
>
> 中譯為編者自譯。

(Stanza 2 line 8: the source file reads "From age to age the same"; the hymnal's printed text is the standard "From age to age the same" — the Wikisource transcription has a stray "to"; I used the standard reading above. Verify against the second witness listed in the report if you want to keep strict verbatim-from-file.)

## ch.4 (Nah 3:1-7) — Alas! and Did My Saviour Bleed

> **Alas! and Did My Saviour Bleed** \
> Isaac Watts; text from *The Army and Navy Hymnal* (1920), stanzas 1, 2 (syllable hyphens of the transcription removed; Hudson refrain omitted)
>
> Alas! and did my Saviour bleed, \
> And did my Sovereign die? \
> Would he devote that sacred head \
> For such an one as I?
>
> 哀哉！我的救主竟然流血， \
> 我的君王竟然受死？ \
> 祂竟肯獻上那神聖的頭， \
> 為了像我這樣的人？
>
> Was it for crimes that I have done, \
> He groaned upon the tree? \
> Amazing pity, grace unknown, \
> And love beyond degree!
>
> 難道是為了我所犯的罪， \
> 祂在木架上呻吟？ \
> 奇妙的憐憫，未曾知曉的恩典， \
> 無可度量的愛！
>
> 中譯為編者自譯。

## ch.5 (Nah 3:8-19) — Our God, Our Help in Ages Past

> **Our God, Our Help in Ages Past** \
> Isaac Watts (1719); text from *The Army and Navy Hymnal* (1920), stanzas 1, 2, 5 (as printed there)
>
> Our God, our help in ages past, \
> Our hope for years to come, \
> Our shelter from the stormy blast, \
> And our eternal home!
>
> 我們的神，歷世歷代的幫助， \
> 是我們將來歲月的盼望， \
> 是我們躲避狂風的避所， \
> 也是我們永遠的家！
>
> Under the shadow of thy throne \
> Thy saints have dwelt secure; \
> Sufficient is thine arm alone, \
> And our defence is sure.
>
> 在你寶座的蔭庇之下， \
> 你的聖徒安然居住； \
> 單單你的膀臂已經足夠， \
> 我們的保障穩妥可靠。
>
> Time, like an ever-rolling stream, \
> Bears all its sons away; \
> They fly, forgotten, as a dream \
> Dies at the opening day.
>
> 時間像川流不息的大河， \
> 把它所有的兒女帶走； \
> 他們飛逝，被人忘記，如同夢境 \
> 在天破曉時消散。
>
> 中譯為編者自譯。
