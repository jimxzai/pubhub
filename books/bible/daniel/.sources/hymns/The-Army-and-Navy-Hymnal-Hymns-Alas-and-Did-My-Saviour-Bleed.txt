Alas! and Did My Saviour Bleed

221

Isaac Watts, 1674-1748

R. E. Hudson

Alas! and did my Saviour bleed,And did my Sovereign die? 

Would he devote that sacred head For such an one as I ? 

Refrain

At the cross, at the cross,where I first saw the light, 

And the burden Of my heart rolled away, 

It was there by faith I received my sight, 

And now I am happy all the day 

Was it for crimes that I have done, He groaned up-on the tree? 

Amaz-ing pit - y, grace unknown, And love beyond degree ! 

Refrain

But drops of grief can ne'er re-pay The debt of love I owe; 

Here, Lord, I  give my-self away, Tis all that I can do!
