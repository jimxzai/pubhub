# Working Bibliography and Source-Control Plan

This is the editorial bibliography for the Acts study. Edition, page, and quotation-level verification must be completed before release; the source register in [RIGHTS-REGISTER.md](RIGHTS-REGISTER.md) tracks the remaining evidence.

## Scripture and language tools

1. **Chinese Union Version (和合本 / CUV).** Edition and copyright notice to be confirmed against the exact source text used in production. The Hong Kong Bible Society provides historical and revision information at <https://www.hkbs.org.hk/8>; the Taiwan Bible Society provides version information at <https://www.bstwn.org/chinese-bible/>.
2. **New American Standard Bible (NASB).** Lockman Foundation edition to be confirmed against the exact source text and permission terms used in production. Use the official quotation guidance at <https://www.lockman.org/permission-to-quote-copyright-trademark-information/> and record whether the draft uses NASB 1995 or NASB 2020.
3. **Nestle-Aland, Novum Testamentum Graece.** Greek New Testament edition to be recorded for every textual or lexical claim.
4. **Bauer, Danker, Arndt, and Gingrich.** *A Greek-English Lexicon of the New Testament and Other Early Christian Literature* (BDAG). Edition and page/location to be recorded for lexical notes.

## Historical and theological sources

5. John Chrysostom. *Homilies on the Acts of the Apostles*. In *Nicene and Post-Nicene Fathers*, First Series, vol. 11. The CCEL table of contents and digital text are at <https://ccel.org/ccel/schaff/npnf111.html>. Edition, translator, and public-domain/reprint status must still be recorded.
6. John Calvin. *Commentary on the Acts of the Apostles*. CCEL hosts volumes 1 and 2 at <https://www.ccel.org/ccel/calvin/calcom36.toc.html> and <https://www.ccel.org/ccel/calvin/calcom37.toc.html>. Translation and edition must still be recorded.
7. G. Campbell Morgan. *The Acts of the Apostles*. Edition, publisher, year, and page/location to be recorded for every quotation or close paraphrase.
8. John MacArthur. *Acts* sermon and exposition materials, Grace to You. Exact sermon title, date, URL, and quotation permission to be recorded for every use.
9. Flavius Josephus. *Antiquities of the Jews*, especially 19.8.2 and other cited sections. Translation and edition to be recorded.
10. Tacitus. *Annals*, exact book/section and translation to be recorded wherever the draft invokes Tacitean evidence.

## Music and liturgy

11. Each opening hymn and hymn translation must receive an individual credit containing title, author/translator, date, source edition or URL, and rights status. A generic “traditional hymn” label is not sufficient for release.

## Editorial rules

- Distinguish direct quotation, close paraphrase, and original editorial summary.
- Add a chapter-level source note whenever an historical or theological claim is not obvious from Acts itself.
- Do not retain quotation marks around wording that cannot be located in the cited edition.
- Record the final verified source location in the chapter, bibliography, and rights register consistently.
