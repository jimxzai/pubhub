## Minimum release evidence

- A complete bibliography with edition, publisher, year, and page or location for every cited source.
- A copyright page with translation notices and permissions language.
- A signed or archived permission record for any non-public-domain text.
- A final `make release-check` run with no errors.
- PDF, EPUB, and accessible HTML review against the approved source files.
