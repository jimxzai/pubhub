TITLE: Morning (New every morning is the love)
AUTHOR: John Keble
YEAR: not stated on source page (The Christian Year, Cassell edition, 1887 djvu)
SOURCE_URL: https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/The_Christian_Year_(Keble)/Morning
NOTE: Page prints the poem in 4-line stanzas; used the two beginning 'New every morning is the love' and 'New mercies, each returning day' (the second continues across a page break). Indentation of alternate lines removed.

New every morning is the love
Our wakening and uprising prove;
Through sleep and darkness safely brought,
Restored to life, and power, and thought.

New mercies, each returning day,
Hover around us while we pray;
New perils past, new sins forgiven,
New thoughts of God, new hopes of Heaven.
