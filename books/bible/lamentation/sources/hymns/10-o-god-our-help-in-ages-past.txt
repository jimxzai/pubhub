TITLE: Our God, Our Help in Ages Past
AUTHOR: Isaac Watts
YEAR: not stated on source page
SOURCE_URL: https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/The_Army_and_Navy_Hymnal/Hymns/Our_God,_Our_Help_in_Ages_Past
NOTE: Army and Navy Hymnal (1920) text opens 'Our God, our help in ages past' (not 'O God'); stanzas 1 and 2 used.

Our God, our help in ages past,
Our hope for years to come,
Our shelter from the stormy blast,
And our eternal home!

Under the shadow of thy throne
Thy saints have dwelt secure;
Sufficient is thine arm alone,
And our defence is sure.
