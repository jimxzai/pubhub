Source: spurgeon.org (The Spurgeon Center, Midwestern Baptist Theological Seminary) sermon pages, public-domain text of C. H. Spurgeon (1834-1892), saved as page text with a header (AUTHOR, WORK, TEXT, DATE, SOURCE_URL). The spurgeon.org Lamentations scripture index reports 14 sermons (ch.1: 2, ch.2: 1, ch.3: 10, ch.4: 1; one entry counted under Deuteronomy 32). 12 sermons were saved; the list is from a PCA Historical Center index plus searches, so 2 sermons (one tagged ch.2 and one more in ch.3) were not identified and are not here.

Quotable sentences are copied programmatically from the saved file (checked as exact substrings after collapsing whitespace, 60 words or fewer).

## Honest scope

- Spurgeon preached on single verses of Lamentations, nearly all evangelistic or devotional. These are sermon applications, not exposition of the book. Where he reads a verse of Christ (1:12 from the cross; 4:22 of Christ's atonement), that is Spurgeon's application and is labelled so in the note.
- Sermon numbers: printed on the page for 480, 579, 654, 1620, 3083, 3170, 3360, 451. For "How Good to Those Who Seek!" (2436), "The Best Burden for Young Shoulders" (1291), "A Wonder Explained by Greater Wonders" (1812) the number comes from the PCA Historical Center index and is not on the page; for "Solitude, Silence, Submission" (2468) from a search-result title only.
- Dates: 3083, 3170, 3360 are posthumous publication dates. 1291's page gives only the year 1876.
