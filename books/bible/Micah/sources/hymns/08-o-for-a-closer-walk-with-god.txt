TITLE: O for a Closer Walk with God
AUTHOR: William Cowper (marked "C." on source page; Olney Hymns, Book I, Hymn 3)
YEAR: 1840 (Olney Hymns edition, as stated on source page)
SOURCE_URL: https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Olney_Hymns_(1840)/Book_1/Hymn_3

Oh for a closer walk with God,
A calm and heavenly frame,
A light to shine upon the road
That leads me to the Lamb!

Where is the blessedness I knew
When first I saw the Lord?
Where is the soul-refreshing view
Of Jesus and his word?
