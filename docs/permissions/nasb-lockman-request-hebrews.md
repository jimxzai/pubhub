**Nature of the quotation**

The volume is a chapter-by-chapter devotional and expository study of Hebrews.
Each of the thirteen chapters presents the Chinese Union Version (1919, public
domain) text of that chapter, followed by the same chapter in the NASB, set in
matching paragraph divisions so that the two may be read in parallel. The
Scripture text accompanies original commentary, historical annotation, and
study questions; the quoted verses constitute well under half of the total work.

I understand that your guidelines permit quotation of up to 1,000 verses
without written permission provided the quotation does not amount to a complete
book of the Bible. Because this volume presents Hebrews in full, I am writing
to request express written permission for that use.

We would of course carry the required notice:

> Scripture quotations taken from the New American Standard Bible® (NASB),

> Used by permission. www.Lockman.org
